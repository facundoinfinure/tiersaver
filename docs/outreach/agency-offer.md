## Objections and answers

<!-- PUBLIC TEXT START -->

**"We already do this by hand."**
Then you know the routine, and the app adds nothing you could not do yourself. What it changes is the repetition: the same criteria applied every month on every store, a check of each profile against Shopify orders, a simulation someone signs off, and a log you can show the client. If your manual process already covers those four things for every account every month, you probably do not need it, and I would like to hear how you run it.

**"Klaviyo has native suppression recommendations. Why an app?"**
It does, and they are good: Klaviyo suggests profiles to suppress on the Suppressed profiles page and lets you suppress a segment in a few clicks. TierSaver uses the criteria Klaviyo publishes. What it adds around them: a cross-check against the store's Shopify order history, a simulation that shows the reason for each group before anything changes, a log of every run, a Restore button for a whole cleanup, and a monthly schedule so it does not depend on someone remembering.

**"Will it suppress buyers?"**
By default a profile is only a candidate if it never opened, clicked, visited or purchased, and every candidate is checked against Shopify orders as well as Klaviyo. If the order data does not cover the period, the profile stays active. You see the simulation and a CSV sample before approving.

**"What if we get it wrong?"**
Each cleanup can be restored from its page in the app, within limits the help center explains. One comes from Klaviyo: after a suppression is removed, that profile cannot be manually suppressed again until 90 days after the previous suppression. The app tells you before you restore.

**"Does it delete anything?"**
No. It suppresses. Profiles and their history stay in Klaviyo.

**"Does it run on its own?"**
Not at first. The first cleanup is always manual. Afterwards the schedule prepares a new simulation each month and waits for approval. A fully automatic mode exists as a separate opt-in, only with the default criteria.

**"Will my client's Klaviyo plan change?"**
I make no promise about that. Klaviyo documents that suppressed profiles do not count toward the email plan's active profiles, that a plan does not move down by itself, and that on Advanced KDP suppressed profiles still count toward that plan's total.

**"We run sunset flows."**
Keep them. A sunset flow is a last attempt to re-engage. TierSaver deals with the profiles that never engaged at all, and it does not send email.

**"What data do you keep?"**
Klaviyo profile IDs, keyed hashes, dates and statuses. No email addresses in clear. Tokens are encrypted, and everything is removed when the app is uninstalled.

**"Is there a partner program?"**
Not yet. I would like to design it with the first agencies that use the app, and I would tell you plainly what is and is not decided.

**"Who is behind it?"**
I'm Facundo, the founder and the only person working on it. TierSaver is a product of Digital Solvers LLC. It is new and has no customers yet. That is why I'm asking for feedback, not for a contract.

## 15-minute demo script

En fase 1 la demo corre sobre datos de demostración (tienda de desarrollo y cuenta de Klaviyo de prueba). En fase 2 puede correr sobre una cuenta que elija la agencia.

<!-- PUBLIC TEXT START -->

**0:00 to 2:00 · Their process first.** "Before I show anything: how do you handle never-engaged profiles across client accounts today? Who does it, and how often?" Listen. Write down their words.

**2:00 to 3:00 · One sentence.** "TierSaver keeps a Klaviyo list clean for a Shopify store: it suppresses profiles that never engaged, using the criteria Klaviyo publishes, after a simulation someone approves." Say what you are looking at: demo data, or the account they chose.

**3:00 to 5:00 · The audit.** Show active profiles, how many meet the default criteria, and how many are protected and why. Say: "The audit changed no profile. It added one segment to the Klaviyo account, and Klaviyo evaluates it."

**5:00 to 8:00 · The simulation.** Open it. Show the count to be suppressed and the reason, the protected groups, and the CSV sample. Point at the Shopify order check: "Anyone with an order stays, whatever Klaviyo recorded."

**8:00 to 10:00 · Approval, log, Restore.** Show the approval step, then History and a cleanup's page with Restore. Mention the 90-day limit Klaviyo applies after a suppression is removed.

**10:00 to 11:00 · Schedule and price.** Monthly simulation that waits for approval. The plan by list size, $9 to $79 a month, starting only at the first approved cleanup and renewing every 30 days until cancelled.

**11:00 to 14:00 · Their verdict.** "What is missing for you to use this on a client account? What would you not trust? What would you want to show your client afterwards?" Do not defend. Write it down.

**14:00 to 15:00 · One next step.** Before approval: "May I write to you the day it is approved?" After approval: "Which account should we audit first?" Nothing else.

**Do not say:** anything about what the client pays Klaviyo, any number that is not on the screen, the name of another app, or a commission.

Formas posibles de un acuerdo con agencias, para elegir más adelante:

| Opción | Qué sería | A favor | En contra o a resolver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Sin programa | La agencia la usa porque le sirve; no hay pago en ningún sentido | Simple; ningún conflicto de interés | Menos incentivo para recomendar |
| B. Beneficio para el cliente | Un período sin cargo para las tiendas que trae la agencia | No hay dinero hacia la agencia | Hay que ver si el cobro por Shopify lo permite y cómo se describe en la ficha |
| C. Pago por referido | Un porcentaje o monto fijo por tienda que paga | Incentivo claro | Requiere contrato, facturación e impuestos; la agencia tiene que decírselo a su cliente; revisar las reglas de Shopify y de Klaviyo para partners |
| D. Vista multi-cuenta o informe con marca de la agencia | Producto, no dinero | Es lo que más piden las agencias, en teoría | No existe; está fuera del alcance del MVP (`docs/01-PRD.md`) |
| E. Co-diseño | Las primeras agencias definen qué necesita el modo agencia | Buena información | No es un beneficio económico |

Regla que vale para cualquier opción con dinero o beneficios: **se informa**. La agencia que cobra o recibe algo por recomendar la app se lo dice a su cliente, y si lo recomienda en público lo aclara (guías de la FTC sobre recomendaciones; ver `README.md`). TierSaver lo pide por escrito en el acuerdo. Nunca se ofrece nada a cambio de una reseña en el App Store.

No se ofrece en ningún borrador.
