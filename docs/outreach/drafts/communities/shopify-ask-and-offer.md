**Title:** Looking for feedback: an app that keeps a Klaviyo list clean (free audit, built by me)

Hi all. I'm Facundo, and I built TierSaver, a Shopify app for stores that use Klaviyo. I'm looking for honest feedback from a few merchants before going wider.

What it does:

- It finds the profiles in your Klaviyo account that never engaged, using the criteria Klaviyo publishes for suppressing unengaged profiles: created at least 365 days ago, sent 5 or more emails over roughly the last 72 weeks, never opened or clicked, no recorded site visit, product view or started checkout, and never purchased.
- It checks every one of those profiles against your Shopify orders, so nobody who bought is touched.
- It shows a simulation first: how many profiles would be suppressed and why, and how many stay protected and why. Nothing is suppressed until you approve.
- Every cleanup is logged and can be restored from the app. It suppresses; it never deletes profiles.

What it costs: installing, connecting Klaviyo, the audit and the simulation are free. A monthly plan by list size ($9 up to 10,000 active profiles, $19 up to 25,000, $39 up to 75,000, $79 up to 150,000) starts only when you approve your first cleanup. It renews every 30 days through your Shopify invoice until you cancel in Settings or uninstall.

What the audit touches: it changes no profile. It adds one segment to your Klaviyo account, which Klaviyo uses to evaluate the criteria.

What I'm asking: run the free audit and tell me what was unclear, what you did not trust, and what you would need to see before approving a cleanup. You can do the whole routine by hand in Klaviyo too, and I'm glad to explain how in this thread.
