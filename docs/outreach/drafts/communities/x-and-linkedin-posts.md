## X · hilo de valor (6 posts)

<!-- PUBLIC TEXT START -->

1/ Klaviyo counts as active "any profile that can be emailed through Klaviyo". That includes people who gave an email at checkout and never opted in. Here is how to find and suppress the ones that never engaged, by hand.

2/ Build this segment (from Klaviyo's help center): can receive email marketing, email is set, created at least 180 days ago, received at least 5 emails in the last 72 weeks, and 0 opens, clicks, site activity, product views, checkouts started and orders over all time.

3/ Klaviyo's own suggestions on the Suppressed profiles page use 365 days, not 180. When unsure, use 365.

4/ Before you act: export the segment and compare it with your Shopify orders. Keep every buyer. This step is not in Klaviyo's instructions and it is the one that protects revenue.

5/ Audience > Lists & segments > action menu > "Suppress current members". Suppress, never delete: Klaviyo says deleting "cannot be undone". Keep the export as your record.

6/ The plan does not change by itself. Klaviyo says suppressing "will not automatically change the Email subscription plan on your account". Pick a smaller plan in billing, or turn on auto-downgrade. Repeat monthly. (I built a tool that automates this, TierSaver. The steps work without it.)

## LinkedIn · post de valor (suppress, delete, unsubscribe)

<!-- PUBLIC TEXT START -->

Three Klaviyo words that get mixed up, and why it matters for list hygiene.

Suppress: a status that blocks marketing email to a profile. The profile and its history stay. You can remove it later.

Delete: the profile is gone. Klaviyo says the action "cannot be undone". If the person signs up again, they come back as a new profile with no history.

Unsubscribe: the recipient's own choice. You cannot reverse it, and you should not want to.

For profiles that never opened, clicked, visited or bought, the reversible option is suppress. Export the list first, compare it with your Shopify orders, and keep every buyer.

One more thing people miss: suppressing does not change your Klaviyo plan by itself. Klaviyo says so in its help center. You choose a smaller plan in billing settings, or turn on auto-downgrade.

(Disclosure: I'm the founder of TierSaver, a Shopify app that automates the suppress step. Nothing above needs it.)
