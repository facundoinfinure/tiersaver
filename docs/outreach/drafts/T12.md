## Answer

A calmer way to look at it: Klaviyo's plan follows active profiles, meaning anyone who can be emailed, and Klaviyo documents how to take never-engaged profiles out of that count.

How to do it by hand in Klaviyo:

1. Build a segment of profiles that never engaged. Klaviyo's help center describes one ("Understanding active profile management in Klaviyo"): can receive email marketing, created at least 180 days ago, received at least 5 emails in the last 72 weeks, and zero opens, clicks, site activity, product views, checkouts started and orders. Creating the segment does not suppress anyone.
2. Check it before acting. Open a sample of profiles, and compare against your store's orders so nobody who bought is in it.
3. Go to Audience > Lists & segments, open the menu next to the segment and choose "Suppress current members". If Klaviyo shows a choice, "Suppress inactive profiles" keeps people who clicked or ordered in the last 182 days.
4. Suppress, don't delete. Suppressed profiles keep their history; deleting cannot be undone, and a deleted person can come back as a new profile.
5. Suppressed profiles stop counting toward the email plan's active profiles, but the plan does not change by itself: choose a lower plan in billing, or turn on auto-downgrade.
6. The action only covers current members, so repeat it every month or quarter. One thing to know: if you unsuppress a profile, you cannot suppress it again by hand for 90 days.
