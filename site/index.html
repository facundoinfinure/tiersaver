How it works

Five steps. You approve the one that matters.

Klaviyo publishes criteria for suppressing unengaged profiles. TierSaver applies them for you, with your Shopify orders as an extra check.

  1. 01Audit

    See where your list stands

    Connect Klaviyo and run a free audit. It changes no profile: it adds one segment to your Klaviyo account, which Klaviyo uses to evaluate the criteria, and shows how many active profiles you have and how many meet them.

  2. 02Simulate

    Preview every cleanup

    A simulation shows how many profiles would be suppressed and why, and lets you download the full list to check it. Nothing is suppressed until you approve a simulation, and an approval can be used for 24 hours. Each profile is checked again right before it is suppressed, so a cleanup can suppress fewer profiles than you approved.

  3. 03Protect buyers

    Keep anyone with recorded interest

    A profile is only a candidate if every one of these is true. Purchases are checked against both your Shopify orders and Klaviyo. Orders are read every hour and before each audit; if that data is not up to date, every profile is kept.

    • Created 365+ days ago
    • Received 5+ emails
    • Never opened
    • Never clicked
    • Never visited
    • Never bought

    SMS subscribers, lists you protect and profiles with incomplete data are always kept.

  4. 04Suppress

    Repeat it monthly, if you choose

    You start the first cleanup yourself. The monthly schedule is off by default. If you turn it on, TierSaver runs a new audit every month, a few days before your Klaviyo plan renews, because Klaviyo updates its count on its own schedule. Each scheduled cleanup waits for your approval. Automatic mode is a separate opt-in: it only repeats the default criteria, and only after you have approved and run a cleanup yourself. It still waits for your review when a cleanup does not look like the one you last approved.

  5. 05Undo

    Correct a cleanup

    If a cleanup suppressed someone it should not have, Restore removes the suppression that cleanup added. Restored profiles are left out of cleanups for the next 365 days. What Restore does, and its limits.

Questions

It touches your list, so ask the hard questions.

More detail in the help center, or write to support@tiersaver.com.

Will I lose customers?

Buyers found in your store's order history are kept. A profile that TierSaver can match to a purchase, in your Shopify orders or in Klaviyo, is not a candidate. Matching uses the email address on the order, so a purchase made under a different address cannot be matched. By default a candidate has also never opened, clicked or visited.

You approve a simulation before anything is suppressed, you can download the list to check it, and a cleanup can be restored from the app afterwards. See the cleanup criteria.

Does the audit change anything in my Klaviyo account?

It changes no profile. It creates one segment in your account, named "TierSaver: long-term inactive profiles (managed, do not edit)", which Klaviyo uses to evaluate the criteria. Nothing is suppressed until you approve a simulation.

How the audit works.

Is my data deleted?

No. TierSaver suppresses profiles; it does not delete them. Their history stays in Klaviyo.

What suppressing means in Klaviyo.

Can I still email someone after they are suppressed?

Not with marketing email. A suppressed profile stops receiving your campaigns and most flow emails, even if that person later buys. Flows that Klaviyo has approved as transactional still send, and SMS and push are not affected. If the person subscribes again, Klaviyo removes the suppression.

That is why the default criteria only select profiles with no recorded engagement of any kind. What changes after a cleanup.

Can a cleanup be reversed?

Yes, from the cleanup's page in the app, with the Restore button. It removes the manual suppression that cleanup added. It needs the app installed and connected to Klaviyo, it does not send the emails those profiles missed in the meantime, and profiles that unsubscribed or bounced stay unsendable in Klaviyo. It leaves alone profiles that were already suppressed before the cleanup and customers whose data was erased on request, and it may not be able to tell its own suppression apart from one you added yourself.

What Restore does, and its limits.

Doesn't Klaviyo already do this?

Klaviyo publishes criteria for suppressing unengaged profiles and gives you the tools to do it by hand. TierSaver applies those criteria for you, adds a check against your Shopify orders, shows you a simulation first and keeps a log so a cleanup can be restored.

If you prefer to do it yourself, the manual steps are here.

When am I charged?

Not until you approve your first cleanup. Installing, connecting Klaviyo, the audit and the simulation are free. When you approve your first cleanup, Shopify asks you to approve the monthly plan for your list size. From then on Shopify charges it every 30 days, whether or not a cleanup runs that month, until you cancel in Settings or uninstall. The period already paid is not refunded, and restoring a cleanup does not refund the month.

How pricing works.

Does a cleaner list change my Klaviyo plan?

Not by itself. Klaviyo's help center says that suppressed profiles do not count toward your email plan's active profile count, and that a lower active profile count does not necessarily lower your bill; on Klaviyo's Advanced KDP plan suppressed profiles still count toward its total. If your count drops, changing plans is your decision, made in Klaviyo's billing settings. TierSaver never changes your plan, and makes no promise about your Klaviyo invoice.

The monthly schedule and your Klaviyo plan.

What happens if I uninstall?

Profiles that were suppressed stay suppressed. You can remove the suppression from Klaviyo at any time.

TierSaver removes its access tokens when you uninstall and the rest of your store's data when Shopify sends its deletion request, or 10 days after the uninstall if that request has not arrived. What happens on uninstall.

What does TierSaver store about my customers?

Klaviyo profile IDs and keyed hashes of email addresses, computed with a key unique to your store, so it can recognize buyers and restore a cleanup. It does not store customer email addresses, names or order contents. Access tokens are encrypted.
