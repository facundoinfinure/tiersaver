2. What the app does

  • With the access you grant, it reads data from your Klaviyo account and your Shopify orders to find profiles that have never engaged or, if you choose wider criteria, have not engaged within the periods you set. The default criteria follow the criteria Klaviyo publishes for suppressing unengaged profiles, with extra protections added by the app. The wider criteria are TierSaver's own optional setting: they go beyond the criteria Klaviyo publishes and suppress more profiles. You can review the criteria in the app.
  • It creates and maintains one segment in your Klaviyo account, which it uses to find those profiles. The audit itself does not suppress or edit any profile.
  • It computes a simulation before every cleanup. Unless you have turned on automatic mode, nothing changes until you have reviewed and approved that simulation.
  • After you approve a simulation and start the cleanup, it asks Klaviyo to suppress the profiles in that simulation from email. Each profile is checked again right before it is sent, and a profile that has bought since the audit, no longer meets the criteria in Klaviyo, has changed its email address or is already suppressed is left untouched. A cleanup can therefore suppress fewer profiles than the simulation you approved. Other profiles may be left untouched because they no longer exist or because the cleanup stops early. You can stop a cleanup while it runs; the batch being sent at that moment, up to 100 profiles, is still sent. A cleanup that stops responding is closed by the app with a record of what was sent. The first cleanup always needs your approval. If you later turn on automatic mode, you approve in advance the monthly cleanups described in sections 3 and 6.
  • It keeps a record of each cleanup, including who approved it, and lets you undo a cleanup from the app to correct a mistake, within the limits described in section 4.

The app suppresses profiles. It never deletes profiles. It does not send email or other messages to your customers, it does not obtain, record or change consent, it does not process unsubscribe requests, and it does not change your Klaviyo plan or billing settings.

4. Suppression and its effects

What suppression does in Klaviyo

Suppression applies to email. A suppressed profile is skipped by your Klaviyo campaigns and flows, including welcome, abandoned cart, back-in-stock and win-back messages, even if that person later buys, until the suppression is removed. According to Klaviyo's documentation on November 26, 2026, emails that Klaviyo has approved as transactional can still be sent to a manually suppressed profile, suppression does not change SMS or push consent, and Klaviyo removes a manual suppression if the person subscribes to your email marketing again. Suppression can also change the size of your segments, the reach of your flows, your reports and any audience you sync from Klaviyo to another service. Klaviyo decides how suppression works and can change it at any time.

You also understand that:

  • the app selects profiles using the data available to it, and that data can be incomplete or wrong. Buyers are recognized by the email address on their Shopify orders and by the purchases Klaviyo has on record, so a purchase made under a different address, or one that neither service has on record, cannot be matched. The app reads your Shopify orders every hour and before each audit, and keeps every profile active if that data is not up to date when an audit runs;
  • Klaviyo applies its own rules, including a waiting period before a profile that was unsuppressed can be suppressed again;
  • when you uninstall the app, suppressed profiles stay suppressed until you remove the suppression in Klaviyo.

A suppression is not an unsubscribe

A suppression applied by the app is a list hygiene measure. It is not a record of an unsubscribe or of an objection to marketing, it does not replace one, and you must not rely on it to honor one. If a person asks you to stop emailing them, record that in Klaviyo as an unsubscribe, whether or not the app has suppressed their profile.

What undo does, and its limits

Undo asks Klaviyo to remove the manual suppression that the cleanup added. It covers the profiles Klaviyo accepted and the profiles that were sent to Klaviyo without a confirmed answer. It leaves untouched a profile that Klaviyo shows as suppressed since before the cleanup, the profile of a customer whose data was erased at that customer's request, and a profile that no longer exists or has no email address. You understand that:

  • Klaviyo records only one kind of manual suppression and does not say who added it. Undo removes the suppression the cleanup added, and the app may not be able to tell it apart from one that you, a colleague or another tool added to the same profile for your own reason. If that happened, undo may remove that suppression too;
  • undo does not resubscribe anyone. According to Klaviyo's documentation on November 26, 2026, profiles that unsubscribed, bounced or have an invalid address stay as they are, and Klaviyo keeps them unsendable;
  • undo does not restore emails that were not sent in the meantime, and it cancels a charge only in the case described in section 6;
  • a profile restored by an undo is left out of later cleanups by the app for 365 days;
  • undo needs the app to be installed and connected to your Klaviyo account, and it may restore only part of a cleanup, for example when a profile no longer exists.

After an undo the restored profiles can receive your marketing email again, and you are responsible for checking that you may lawfully email them before you do.
