2. What the app does

  • With the access you grant, it reads data from your Klaviyo account and your Shopify orders to find profiles that have never engaged, using cleanup criteria you can review.
  • It creates and maintains one segment in your Klaviyo account, which it uses to find those profiles.
  • It shows you a simulation of each cleanup before anything changes.
  • After you approve a simulation, it suppresses the profiles in that simulation from email in your Klaviyo account. The first cleanup always needs your approval. If you later turn on automatic mode, you approve in advance the monthly cleanups described in section 3.
  • It keeps a record of each cleanup, including who approved it, and lets you undo a cleanup from the app to correct a mistake.

The app suppresses profiles. It never deletes profiles. It does not send email or other messages to your customers, it does not change consent, and it does not change your Klaviyo plan or billing settings.

4. Suppression and its effects

A suppressed profile cannot receive marketing emails from your Klaviyo account, even if that person later buys, until the suppression is removed. You understand that:

  • Klaviyo applies its own rules, including a waiting period before a profile that was unsuppressed can be suppressed again;
  • undoing a cleanup removes the suppression the app applied, but does not restore emails that were not sent in the meantime;
  • when you uninstall the app, suppressed profiles stay suppressed until you remove the suppression in Klaviyo.
