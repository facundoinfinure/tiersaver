2. What the app does

  • With the access you grant, it reads data from your Klaviyo account and your Shopify orders to find profiles that have never engaged or, if you choose wider criteria, have not engaged within the periods you set. You can review the criteria in the app.
  • It creates and maintains one segment in your Klaviyo account, which it uses to find those profiles.
  • It computes a simulation before every cleanup. Unless you have turned on automatic mode, nothing changes until you have reviewed and approved that simulation.
  • After you approve a simulation and start the cleanup, it asks Klaviyo to suppress the profiles in that simulation from email. Some may be left untouched, for example a profile that no longer exists or a cleanup that stops early. The first cleanup always needs your approval. If you later turn on automatic mode, you approve in advance the monthly cleanups described in section 3.
  • It keeps a record of each cleanup, including who approved it, and lets you undo a cleanup from the app to correct a mistake.

The app suppresses profiles. It never deletes profiles. It does not send email or other messages to your customers, it does not change consent, and it does not change your Klaviyo plan or billing settings.

4. Suppression and its effects

A suppressed profile cannot receive marketing emails from your Klaviyo account, even if that person later buys, until the suppression is removed. You understand that:

  • the app selects profiles using the data available to it, and that data can be incomplete or wrong;
  • Klaviyo applies its own rules, including a waiting period before a profile that was unsuppressed can be suppressed again;
  • undoing a cleanup removes the suppression the app applied, but does not restore emails that were not sent in the meantime;
  • a profile restored by an undo is left out of later cleanups by the app for 365 days;
  • undo needs the app to be installed and connected to your Klaviyo account;
  • when you uninstall the app, suppressed profiles stay suppressed until you remove the suppression in Klaviyo.

  • Currency and taxes. Prices are stated in US dollars and exclude taxes, which Shopify may add.

Undo and refunds. A charge is created in Shopify 72 hours after a cleanup finishes, once Klaviyo has confirmed the suppressions. If you undo the cleanup in full during those 72 hours, so that every profile it suppressed has been restored before the charge is created, the cleanup is not charged. In every other case, including an undo of only part of a cleanup and an undo after the charge has been created, undoing a cleanup does not cancel or refund its charge. Charges that have been created are not refundable, except where the law requires a refund. If you believe a charge was made in error, write to support@tiersaver.com.
