Settings: criteria, protected lists and the monthly schedule

The Settings page, section by section. Where there is a default, it is the cautious choice.

Last updatedNovember 26, 2026

Who counts as inactive

By default TierSaver uses the criteria Klaviyo publishes for suppressing unengaged profiles: a profile created at least 365 days ago that was sent 5 or more emails in about 72 weeks and never opened, clicked, visited your site or purchased. Profiles with SMS consent, in a protected list or segment, or restored in the last 365 days are always kept active.

Ticking the wider-windows box replaces "never" with time windows. This is TierSaver's own optional setting. It goes beyond the criteria Klaviyo publishes and suppresses more profiles, because it can also select people who engaged or bought in the past. It shows these fields:

  • Purchased, clicked, opened, was on your site in the last (days). One field each. A profile with that activity inside the window is kept active. Leave a field empty for "ever". The shortest windows allowed are 365 days for purchases and 182 days for the others.
  • Created or subscribed in the last (days). Newer profiles are kept active. Between 180 and 1095 days.
  • Emails a profile must have received. Between 5 and 50. Nobody is inactive if you never wrote to them.
  • Ignore opens that Klaviyo flags as automatic (Apple Mail Privacy Protection). Off by default: every open keeps a profile active.

Automatic cleanups are not available with wider windows. See cleanup criteria for the reasoning.

Protected lists and segments

Profiles in a protected list or segment are never suppressed, whatever their activity. Tick the lists and segments you want to protect.

  • You can protect up to 20 lists and segments.
  • The page shows the first 50 lists and the first 50 segments of your Klaviyo account.
  • If a protected list or segment cannot be read during an audit, every profile is kept active.
  • A protected list or segment that Klaviyo no longer returns stays in your settings, marked as not found, and stays protected until you untick it.
  • Klaviyo has to be connected to choose lists and segments.

Monthly schedule

Off by default. See the monthly schedule and your Klaviyo plan for how the day is chosen.

  • Run the audit every month. Turns the schedule on or off. Turning it off takes effect at once and does not undo anything that already ran.
  • Day of the month your Klaviyo plan renews. A day from 1 to 31. Leave it empty to use the day Klaviyo reported in your last audit, when one is available.
  • Days before the renewal to run the audit. Between 4 and 14. The default is 5.

Automatic cleanups

Off by default. With it off, each scheduled audit waits for your approval for 24 hours. Automatic mode lets the scheduled cleanup start without a separate approval each month. You turn it on by ticking the box in this section, which also accepts the monthly plan and TierSaver's terms of service; the app records who turned it on, when, the price shown and the version of the terms. The app only accepts it when all of these hold:

  • the monthly schedule is on;
  • you use the default criteria, not wider windows;
  • you have already approved and run at least one cleanup yourself. Until then, scheduled simulations keep waiting for your approval.

Even then, an automatic cleanup waits for your review when it does not look like the one you last approved: when that cleanup was restored in full, when your criteria or protected lists changed since, or when it would suppress more than 10% of your active profiles or more than three times as many profiles as the last cleanup you approved. See simulation and approval.

Automatic cleanups are covered by your monthly plan: nothing extra is charged per cleanup. They only start while your plan is active and covers your list size; if your list has grown past your plan, the cleanup waits until you approve the new monthly price in Shopify. Each one is listed under History. The app does not send email about it. To stop it, untick the box or turn the schedule off.

Plan

This section shows your TierSaver plan: its price, the list size it covers and the date the current period ends. Before your first cleanup it says "No plan yet. It starts when you approve your first cleanup."

  • Cancel plan. Shopify stops charging the plan; the period you already paid for is not refunded. No cleanup can start, and automatic cleanups are turned off. Scheduled audits keep running and stay free. Profiles already suppressed stay suppressed, and you can still restore them from History. A new plan starts if you approve another cleanup.
  • Your list has grown into a larger plan. Your next cleanup waits until you approve the new price in Shopify. Until then you keep paying your current price.
  • Your list now fits a smaller plan. The app offers to switch. It is your choice: Shopify asks you to approve the new price and prorates the change.
  • Your plan is on hold in Shopify. Shopify put it on hold because of a payment problem with your store. Cleanups cannot start until Shopify makes it active again.

See billing and your plan.

Customer data requests

When one of your customers asks your store for their data, Shopify sends the request to TierSaver. The request is listed in this section for 90 days, with a Download button. The file holds what TierSaver stores that is linked to that customer's email address: whether an order is on record, and the cleanups that included their Klaviyo profile. Pass it on to your customer; the app does not send it for you. See data and privacy.

When you change a setting

Changes apply when you confirm them with the button at the bottom of the page. If you change the criteria or the protected lists and segments, simulations that have not run yet are discarded: run the audit again before the next cleanup. Changing only the schedule does not discard them. If a value is outside its limits, the page says which one and nothing is changed.
