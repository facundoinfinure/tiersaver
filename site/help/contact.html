Help center

Contact support

Support is by email. A person reads every message.

Last updatedNovember 26, 2026

Where to write

  • Help with the app: support@tiersaver.com
  • Everything else, including privacy and legal questions, and prices for lists above 150,000 active profiles: hello@tiersaver.com

We answer by email. We do not commit to a response time.

What to include

  • Your store's myshopify.com address.
  • What you were doing and the message the app showed, word for word if you can.
  • If it is about a specific audit or cleanup, its date. A cleanup's page in History has it.

What not to send

  • Customer email addresses or exports of your list.
  • Passwords, API keys or access tokens. We never need them.

Before you write

Troubleshooting explains the messages the app shows. The rest of the help center covers how each part of the app works.
