Undo: restoring a cleanup

A cleanup can be restored from the app. Undo exists to correct a mistake: it removes the suppression from the profiles of that cleanup.

Last updatedNovember 26, 2026

How to restore a cleanup

  1. Open TierSaver and go to History.
  2. Open the cleanup you want to correct.
  3. In the section "Undo this cleanup", select the Restore button.

Restore becomes available once the cleanup has finished and Klaviyo has finished applying it. If you try too early, the app asks you to try again in a minute or two. The restore then appears in History as its own entry.

What Restore does

  • It removes the manual suppression from the profiles that cleanup suppressed, so they can receive marketing email again.
  • It does not change consent. Profiles that unsubscribed, bounced or have an invalid address stay unsendable in Klaviyo.
  • It does not touch profiles from other cleanups, or profiles that were never part of a TierSaver cleanup.

The limits of Restore

  • It needs the app installed and connected to Klaviyo. After you uninstall, remove suppressions in Klaviyo instead.
  • It cannot always tell your own suppressions apart. Klaviyo records only one kind of manual suppression and does not say who added it. Restore leaves a profile untouched when Klaviyo's dates show that its suppression was already there before the cleanup, or was added again afterwards. When the dates do not settle it, the suppression is removed. So if you manually suppressed one of those same profiles yourself after the cleanup, Restore can lift that too. Check before you restore.
  • Emails are not sent later. Whatever those profiles did not receive while they were suppressed is not sent to them afterwards.
  • The charge. A cleanup you restore in full within 72 hours of its end is not charged. Restoring later, or restoring only part of it, does not refund the charge. See how pricing works.
  • Some profiles may be left as they are. Each profile is checked again right before the restore, and one that no longer exists in Klaviyo or has no email address cannot be restored. The restore's page shows how many were left untouched and why.

What happens to restored profiles

  • They count as active profiles again. Klaviyo's displayed count may take up to 72 hours to refresh after a large change.
  • TierSaver leaves them alone for 365 days. A restored profile is left out of every cleanup during that time. If you restore a cleanup, you are telling TierSaver those profiles should stay.
  • Klaviyo has its own waiting period. Its help center says that once a profile is suppressed and then unsuppressed, it cannot be manually suppressed again until 90 days after the previous suppression. The rule is not active during the first 30 days after a new Klaviyo account is created.

What undo is not for

Undo is not a way to suppress profiles for a while and bring them back later. Suppression is a decision to stop emailing someone. If you expect to email a group again, protect it instead: mark the list or segment as protected in Settings and it will not be selected.

If your Klaviyo plan has renewed, or may have renewed, since the cleanup, the app asks you to confirm that you are restoring the profiles to correct a mistake. Suppressing profiles before a renewal and restoring them afterwards is against TierSaver's terms.

The cleanup log

Each cleanup is recorded: when it started and finished, whether you started it or the schedule did, who approved it, the criteria in effect and how many profiles were suppressed. For each profile the log keeps the Klaviyo profile ID and the reason it was selected. That record is what Restore works from. See History.
