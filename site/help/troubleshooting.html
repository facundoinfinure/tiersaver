Troubleshooting

The messages the app can show, grouped by where you see them. In every case below, nothing is suppressed that you did not approve.

Last updatedNovember 26, 2026

Connecting Klaviyo

  • "Klaviyo was not connected because the request was declined on Klaviyo." The request was declined on Klaviyo's screen. Select "Connect Klaviyo" again and approve the access. What it asks for is in permissions.
  • "Klaviyo could not be connected. Please try again." The connection did not complete. Start again from "Connect Klaviyo"; the link the app gives you is valid for ten minutes, so do it in one go. If it keeps failing, contact support.
  • "Reconnect Klaviyo". TierSaver's access to your Klaviyo account has ended or was removed in Klaviyo. Cleanups are paused until you connect again with "Reconnect Klaviyo" on the home page. Nothing else changes.

The audit

When the app says "The last audit could not finish", it adds the reason. Nothing was changed.

  • Access to Klaviyo has ended. Reconnect Klaviyo and run the audit again.
  • The TierSaver segment in Klaviyo was edited, or deactivated. Run the audit again; it restores the segment. Leave the segment named "TierSaver: long-term inactive profiles (managed, do not edit)" as it is.
  • Klaviyo is still preparing the data. Run the audit again in a few minutes.
  • The list is larger than the audit can read right now. Write to support.
  • Any other case. Something went wrong while reading from Klaviyo. Run the audit again.

If your Klaviyo account has two segments with TierSaver's name, the app cannot tell which one is its own and uses neither. Delete the copy in Klaviyo and run the audit again.

The audit found nobody to suppress

  • "Your list is already clean." No profile matches the cleanup criteria. There is nothing to suppress.
  • Profiles could not be checked against your store's full order history. They were kept active. This happens while your Shopify order history is still being read, or when Shopify has not shared the whole history with the app. A profile is only suppressed once it can be checked against your orders. If the app says the history is still being read, run the audit again later.
  • Your Klaviyo account has no record of delivered emails, opens or clicks yet. Without that data no profile can be called inactive.
  • A protected list or segment could not be read. Every profile was kept active. Run the audit again later.

The section "Kept active even though they meet the criteria" shows how many profiles were kept for each reason.

Simulations

  • "This simulation has expired." A simulation can be approved and run for 24 hours. Run the audit again from the home page.
  • "This simulation is out of date", or "Your rules or protected lists changed after this audit." You changed the criteria or a protected list after it was computed, or a newer audit replaced it. Run the audit again.
  • "This simulation was already used for a cleanup." A simulation runs once. Run the audit again for a new one.
  • The approval is refused. Tick the box that confirms you have read the notices and accept the terms. If the app says it could not identify your Shopify user, reload the page and try again.
  • A scheduled cleanup in automatic mode is waiting for your review. The app says why: the last cleanup you approved was restored, your criteria or protected lists changed since, this cleanup would suppress more than 10% of your active profiles or more than three times as many profiles as the last one you approved, or your list has grown past your plan and the new monthly price needs your approval in Shopify. Review the simulation and approve it yourself if it is right.
  • "The file could not be prepared." The CSV of candidates is read from Klaviyo at the moment you download it. Try again; Klaviyo has to be connected.

Starting a cleanup

  • "You need an active TierSaver plan to start a cleanup." Start the plan from the "Review cleanup" page; Shopify asks you to approve it. See billing and your plan.
  • "Your list has grown into a larger plan." Approve the new monthly price in Shopify before starting the cleanup. Nothing more is charged without that approval.
  • "Your TierSaver plan is on hold in Shopify because of a billing issue with your store." Cleanups can start again once Shopify makes the plan active again. Check your store's billing in Shopify.
  • "Lists this large are not sold automatically." Your list is above 150,000 active profiles. Write to hello@tiersaver.com.
  • "Shopify could not be asked about your plan." Nothing was changed. Try again.
  • "Connect Klaviyo before starting a cleanup." The connection was lost after you approved. Reconnect and start again while the simulation is still valid.
  • "Cleanups are not switched on for this installation yet." The installation can audit and simulate but cannot suppress profiles yet. Nothing was changed in Klaviyo. Contact support.

A cleanup stopped early

The cleanup's page shows "This run stopped early" with the reason. Profiles it did not reach were left untouched.

  • Access to Klaviyo ended, or Klaviyo was disconnected. Reconnect Klaviyo from the home page, then run a new audit for the profiles that were not reached.
  • The simulation was more than 24 hours old when the cleanup started, or your rules changed after it was approved. Run the audit again and approve the new simulation.
  • Something went wrong while talking to Klaviyo. Check the counts on the page, then run a new audit.
  • The cleanup stopped responding. The app closes it automatically, normally within one to two hours, and its page shows what was really sent. You can restore it, or run a new audit for the profiles it did not reach.
  • You selected "Stop cleanup". The batch being sent at that moment, up to 100 profiles, still went through. Profiles already sent stay suppressed and can be restored.

A cleanup that shows "Completed with issues" finished, but some profiles were not processed because of an error. History explains the counts.

Restoring

  • "Klaviyo is still applying this cleanup." Try the restore again in a minute or two.
  • "This cleanup is still running." You can restore it once it has finished.
  • "A restore of this cleanup is already in progress." Wait for it to finish.
  • "There is nothing left to restore from this cleanup." Its profiles were already restored.
  • "Klaviyo is not connected." Reconnect it from the home page first.

Klaviyo's profile count has not changed

Klaviyo applies a cleanup in the background and updates its count on its own schedule, so the count it shows may not change right away. On the cleanup's page, "Check jobs in Klaviyo" shows what Klaviyo reports so far. Your Klaviyo plan does not change automatically unless you enabled auto-downgrade in Klaviyo.

Settings

  • "Your lists and segments could not be read from Klaviyo right now." The ones you already protect are still protected. Open Settings again later.
  • A warning at the top of Settings after you confirm your changes. Nothing was changed. The warning lists what to correct, for example a window shorter than the allowed minimum, or automatic mode without the schedule on.

"Profiles from an earlier cleanup are active again"

See what changes after a cleanup.

What to send to support

Your store's myshopify.com address, the message you see, and the date of the audit or cleanup. Do not send customer email addresses, passwords or API keys. Contact support.
