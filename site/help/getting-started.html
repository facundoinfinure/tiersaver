Getting started: install, connect Klaviyo and run the audit

Four steps take you from installing the app to your first cleanup. Only the last one suppresses anyone, and only after you approve it.

Last updatedNovember 26, 2026

What you need

  • A Shopify store where you can install apps.
  • A Klaviyo account you can sign in to and authorize an app for.

Install the app

TierSaver is a Shopify app that opens inside your Shopify admin. Its Shopify App Store listing is not live yet; until it is, write to hello@tiersaver.com. When you install it, Shopify asks you to allow TierSaver to read your orders. Why it asks for that.

Step 1. Connect Klaviyo

  1. On the app's home page, under "Get started", select "Connect Klaviyo".
  2. Klaviyo opens. Sign in if you are asked to, review the access TierSaver requests and approve it.
  3. You return to the app, which shows "Klaviyo is connected".

TierSaver never asks you to paste a password or an API key. If you decline on Klaviyo's screen, nothing is connected and you can start again whenever you like. What each permission is for is listed in permissions.

Step 2. Run the free audit

Select "Run the free audit". Klaviyo works through your list in the background and the page shows how many profiles have been read so far. You can leave the page and come back.

The audit changes no profile. It creates one segment in your Klaviyo account, which Klaviyo uses to evaluate the criteria. When it finishes, the section "Your list" shows your active profiles, how many meet the criteria and how many a cleanup would suppress. See how the audit works.

Step 3. Review and approve

If the audit found candidates, the home page shows how many profiles are waiting for your review. Open "Review cleanup" to see the simulation, download the list of candidates and approve it. A simulation can be approved and run for 24 hours. See simulation and approval.

Step 4. Run the cleanup

Everything up to here is free and needs no plan. When you approve your first cleanup, Shopify asks you to approve the monthly plan for your list size; from then on Shopify charges it every 30 days until you cancel. Then you start the cleanup yourself. Profiles are suppressed, never deleted. See how pricing works and what changes after a cleanup.

After the first cleanup

  • Every cleanup is listed under History, where it can also be restored.
  • The monthly schedule is off by default. You can turn it on in Settings.
  • If something does not look right, see troubleshooting or contact support.
