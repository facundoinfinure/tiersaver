What happens when you uninstall

Uninstalling stops TierSaver. It does not change your Klaviyo list.

Last updated2026-11-25

Your Klaviyo list

Profiles that were suppressed stay suppressed. TierSaver does not restore anyone when you uninstall. You can remove suppressions from Klaviyo at any time: Klaviyo lets you do it one profile at a time, or in bulk for a list or segment.

If a run needs correcting, undo it before you uninstall. After uninstalling, the app no longer has access to your Klaviyo account and its run log is deleted.

Monthly cleanups stop. Nothing further is suppressed.

Your data

  • Immediately: TierSaver deletes its access tokens for your Shopify store and your Klaviyo account. From that moment it cannot read or change anything.
  • When Shopify sends its shop deletion request: TierSaver deletes everything else it stored for your store, including settings, audits, simulations, run logs and hashed purchase records. Shopify sends that request 48 hours after an app is uninstalled.

What is stored in the first place is listed in data and privacy.

Billing

Charges go through Shopify. After you uninstall, TierSaver creates no further charges.

If only the Klaviyo connection is lost

If TierSaver's access to Klaviyo expires or is withdrawn while the app is still installed, the app shows Klaviyo as disconnected, stops all cleanups and does nothing until you reconnect. Profiles already suppressed stay as they are.
