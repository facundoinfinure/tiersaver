How pricing works

TierSaver has a flat monthly price that depends only on the size of your list. The audit is free, and a month with no cleanup is not charged.

Last updated2026-11-25

Prices

Active profiles in your Klaviyo accountPrice (USD)
Up to 10,000$15 per month
10,001 to 25,000$29 per month
25,001 to 75,000$79 per month
75,001 to 150,000$149 per month
Above 150,000Contact us

Your list size is the number of active profiles in your Klaviyo account: profiles with an email address that are not suppressed.

What is free

  • The audit, as often as you like.
  • Simulations.
  • Any month in which no cleanup runs. If you do not approve a cleanup, or a simulation finds nobody to suppress, there is no charge for that month.

What the price does not depend on

The price depends on your list size and on nothing else. It is not tied to how many profiles are suppressed, and it is not tied to your Klaviyo plan or invoice in any way.

How you are billed

All charges go through Shopify and appear on your Shopify invoice. TierSaver does not ask for a card. You approve the charge in Shopify before the first paid cleanup, and you can cancel by uninstalling the app.

If your list changes size

The price is set by your active profile count when a cleanup runs. For example, a list of 8,000 active profiles is $15 per month, and a list of 12,500 is $29 per month. The app shows which price applies before each cleanup.

For lists above 150,000 active profiles there is no public price: contact us and we will quote your list individually.
