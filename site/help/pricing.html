How pricing works

TierSaver is a monthly plan whose price depends on the size of your list. It is free until your first cleanup: the plan starts when you approve that cleanup, and Shopify then charges it every 30 days until you cancel.

Your list size is the number of active profiles in your Klaviyo account: profiles with an email address that are not suppressed. Prices do not include taxes, which Shopify may add.

Lists above 150,000 active profiles are not sold automatically. There is no public price for them: write to hello@tiersaver.com.

TierSaver is offered only to stores located outside the European Union, the rest of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. A store located there is never charged: the app does not start a plan for it. See where TierSaver is available.

What is free

Installing the app, connecting Klaviyo, the audit and the simulation are free and need no plan. You can run audits as often as you like, before or after you have a plan, and after you cancel one.

The plan, in five lines

  • Price. The monthly price for your list size, from the table above. There is no charge per cleanup, and the price is the same whether a cleanup suppresses a few profiles or many.
  • When it starts. When you approve your first cleanup, on Shopify's approval screen. Nothing is charged before that.
  • How it renews. Shopify charges the plan every 30 days, whether or not a cleanup runs that month, until you cancel.
  • How to cancel. In the app, under Settings, then Plan, or by uninstalling the app. The period already paid is not refunded.
  • Price changes. A higher price only applies after you approve it in Shopify. TierSaver never moves you to a more expensive plan without that approval.

Before you approve your first cleanup, the app shows the price for your list, how it renews and how to cancel. Restoring a cleanup does not refund the month. See undo.

A cleanup needs an active plan

A cleanup, whether you start it or automatic mode does, only starts while your store has an active plan that covers the list size shown in its simulation. Without one, nothing is suppressed.

If your list changes size

  • Your list grows past your plan. The app tells you, and the next cleanup waits until you approve the new monthly price in Shopify. Automatic mode waits too: it never charges more without your approval. Shopify prorates the change. Until you approve, you keep paying your current price.
  • Your list shrinks into a smaller plan. The app tells you and offers the smaller plan. The change is not automatic: you choose it, and approve it in Shopify.

For example, a list of 8,000 active profiles is $9 per month, and a list of 12,500 is $19 per month.

What the price does not depend on

The price depends on your list size. It does not grow with the number of profiles a cleanup suppresses or with the number of cleanups, and it is not tied to your Klaviyo plan or invoice in any way.

How you are billed

The plan is billed by Shopify and appears on your Shopify invoice. TierSaver does not ask for a card. Development stores get a test plan, which Shopify does not bill. The steps, and what happens when a plan is cancelled or put on hold, are in billing and your plan.
