Data and privacy

TierSaver is built to store as little as it can about your customers: identifiers and one-way hashes, not email addresses.

Last updated2026-11-25

What TierSaver stores

DataWhy
Your store's myshopify.com address, time zone and install dateTo identify your store and schedule in your time zone
Shopify and Klaviyo access tokens, encryptedTo read orders and to read and suppress profiles on your behalf
Your Klaviyo account ID and the permissions you grantedTo know which account is connected
Your settings: cleanup criteria, protected lists, renewal day, run modeTo apply your choices
Audit and simulation results: profile counts and the criteria usedTo show you results and keep a history
Klaviyo profile IDs of profiles in a simulation or a run, with the reasonTo suppress exactly what you approved and to undo it
A SHA-256 hash of each buyer's email address, with the date of their last orderTo recognize buyers without keeping their email address
A log of actions: who approved or did what, and whenSo every change is traceable
Billing records: the price band that applied and the charges created through ShopifyTo bill correctly

What TierSaver does not store

  • Customer email addresses in readable form.
  • Customer names, postal addresses or phone numbers.
  • The contents of orders: products, amounts or payment details.
  • The content of your emails or campaigns.

Email addresses pass through TierSaver's systems briefly in two moments: when an order's email is turned into a hash, and when a batch of profiles is sent to Klaviyo for suppression. They are not written to the database or to logs.

How hashes are used

A hash is a one-way fingerprint of a normalized email address. TierSaver compares the fingerprint from a Shopify order with the fingerprint of a Klaviyo profile's email to know that a profile belongs to a buyer. The original address cannot be read back from the stored fingerprint.

Access and permissions

TierSaver requests the smallest set of permissions it needs. In Shopify, it reads orders, and of the protected customer fields only the email address. In Klaviyo, it reads profiles and their activity, and the only change it makes to a profile is its suppression status, for the profiles in a cleanup you approved.

Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. The encryption key is held separately from the database.

Where it runs

The app and its database run on Cloudflare. Charges are handled by Shopify.

Deletion

  • When you uninstall: tokens are deleted immediately, and everything else when Shopify sends its shop deletion request, 48 hours after uninstalling. See what happens on uninstall.
  • When a customer asks your store to erase their data: Shopify notifies TierSaver, which deletes that customer's hashed purchase record and removes their hash from past runs and simulations.
  • When a customer asks for a copy of their data: the only data derived from them is the one-way hash and the date of their last order, so there is nothing readable to export.

Profiles in Klaviyo are never deleted by TierSaver. They are your data, in your Klaviyo account.

This website

tiersaver.com sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts. The list health check runs entirely in your browser; the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
