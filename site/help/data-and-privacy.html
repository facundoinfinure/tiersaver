Data and privacy

TierSaver is built to store as little as it can about your customers: identifiers and hashes, not email addresses.

Last updatedNovember 26, 2026

What TierSaver stores

DataWhy
Your store's myshopify.com address, install date, the Shopify permissions granted, your store's time zone, and from when and up to when your orders have been readTo identify your store, show dates in your time zone and know which orders are covered
Shopify and Klaviyo access and refresh tokens, encryptedTo read orders and to read and suppress profiles on your behalf
Your Klaviyo account ID and the permissions you grantedTo know which account is connected
Your settings: cleanup criteria, protected lists, renewal day, run modeTo apply your choices
Audit and simulation results: profile counts, the usage figures Klaviyo reports and the criteria usedTo show you results and keep a history
Klaviyo profile IDs of profiles in a simulation or a run, with the reason, the status and a keyed hash of the profile's email addressTo suppress exactly what you approved and to undo it. The per-profile list of a simulation that was never run is deleted after 7 days
A keyed hash of each buyer's email address, with the date of their last order and a hash of their Shopify customer IDTo recognize buyers without keeping their email address
A log of actions: who approved or did what (by Shopify user ID), when, and the version of the terms accepted with each manual approval and when automatic mode is turned onSo every change is traceable
The state of your TierSaver plan: Shopify's subscription ID, the monthly price and the list size it covers, its status, whether it is a test plan, and the dates it started, ended and its current period endsTo know whether a cleanup can start and to show you your plan

What TierSaver does not store

  • Customer email addresses in readable form.
  • Customer names, postal addresses or phone numbers.
  • The contents of orders: products, amounts or payment details.
  • The content of your emails or campaigns.

Email addresses pass through TierSaver's systems briefly in four moments: when an order's email is turned into a hash, when an audit turns the email of each profile it evaluates into a hash, when a batch of profiles is checked again and sent to Klaviyo to be suppressed or restored, and when you download the list of profiles in a simulation as a CSV file. They are not written to the database or to logs.

While an audit runs, TierSaver holds working data for each profile it evaluates: the profile ID, the hash of its email address, its dates and its consent and suppression status. It is deleted when the audit finishes or fails, and in any case about 24 hours after the audit started.

How hashes are used

A hash is a fingerprint of a normalized email address. TierSaver compares the fingerprint from a Shopify order with the fingerprint of a Klaviyo profile's email to know that a profile belongs to a buyer.

The fingerprints are keyed hashes (HMAC-SHA-256), computed with a key that is unique to your store. That key is derived from a secret kept outside the database. They are keyed, per-store pseudonymized hashes, not anonymous data: they still relate to a person, so TierSaver treats hashes and Klaviyo profile IDs as personal data. Without the key, a fingerprint cannot be computed from an email address, and the fingerprints of your store cannot be matched with those of another store.

Access and permissions

TierSaver requests only the permissions it uses. In Shopify, it reads orders, and of the protected customer fields only the email address. In Klaviyo, it reads profiles with their dates and their consent and suppression status, it does not read individual opens or clicks, and the only change it makes to a profile is its suppression status, for the profiles in a cleanup you approved. It also creates and keeps one segment in your Klaviyo account, from the first audit, which Klaviyo uses to evaluate the cleanup criteria. The audit changes no profile.

Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. The encryption key is held separately from the database.

Where it runs

The app and its database run on Cloudflare, with the database in the United States (eastern North America). The person who operates and supports TierSaver works from Argentina and can access the data from there for maintenance and support. The plan is billed by Shopify. Cloudflare's per-request logs are switched off for the app. See section 7 of the privacy policy, and the Data Processing Addendum for the terms that cover transfers.

Deletion

  • When you uninstall: tokens are deleted as soon as Shopify notifies TierSaver, and TierSaver asks Klaviyo to revoke its access. Everything else is deleted at the same time. Deleted data can remain for up to 30 days in the hosting provider's point-in-time recovery copies of the database, which cannot be switched off, before it is permanently removed. See what happens on uninstall.
  • When a customer asks your store to erase their data: Shopify notifies TierSaver, which deletes that customer's hashed purchase record, removes them from simulations and removes the link between past cleanups and their Klaviyo profile. The cleanups keep their counts, but a later restore leaves that customer's profile as it is.
  • When a customer asks for a copy of their data: TierSaver holds a keyed hash of their email address, the date of their last order and, if their profile was in a simulation or a cleanup, the Klaviyo profile ID, the reason, the status and the dates. When Shopify sends TierSaver the customer's data request, TierSaver records it and lists it in Settings, under "Customer data requests", where you can download that information for 90 days and pass it on to your customer. The app does not send it for you.

Profiles in Klaviyo are never deleted by TierSaver. They are your data, in your Klaviyo account.

Cookies

The app sets one cookie, __Host-ts_klaviyo_oauth, while you connect your Klaviyo account. It protects that step against forged requests, contains no personal data and lasts at most 10 minutes.

This website

tiersaver.com sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts. The list health check runs entirely in your browser; the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.

Copies and exports
