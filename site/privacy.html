Privacy policy (draft)

What TierSaver collects, why, and how it is deleted. Written to match what the app actually stores.

Last updatedDraft of 2026-11-26

Effective date: November 26, 2026

1. Who we are

TierSaver for Klaviyo ("TierSaver", "the app") is a Shopify app operated by Digital Solvers LLC, 153 E Flagler St, Miami, FL 33131, United States ("we", "us"). You can reach us about privacy at hello@tiersaver.com, and about the app at support@tiersaver.com.

This policy covers the app and the website at tiersaver.com. TierSaver is an independent product and is not affiliated with Klaviyo, Inc. or Shopify Inc.

2. The roles involved

A merchant who installs the app decides which data is processed and why. For the personal data of the merchant's customers, the merchant is the controller (or "business") and we act as a processor (or "service provider") on the merchant's instructions. For data about the merchant's own account and staff, and for visitors to this website, we are the controller.

If you are a customer of a store that uses TierSaver and want to exercise your privacy rights, contact that store. We assist the store in responding.

3. What the app processes

Data we store

  • Store details: the store's myshopify.com domain, time zone, installation status and dates.
  • Access credentials: the Shopify access token and the Klaviyo OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored encrypted, with the permissions granted and the Klaviyo account ID.
  • Settings: cleanup criteria, protected lists or segments (by Klaviyo ID), the plan renewal day, run mode and the Klaviyo plan type.
  • Audit and simulation results: profile counts, the Klaviyo plan size shown as a reference, and the criteria used.
  • Profiles selected for suppression: for each simulation and each run, the Klaviyo profile ID, the reason the profile was selected, its processing status and, for matching purposes, a SHA-256 hash of the profile's email address.
  • Hashed purchase records: for each buyer found in the store's Shopify orders, a SHA-256 hash of the normalized order email address, the date of the most recent order, and a SHA-256 hash of the Shopify customer ID.
  • Activity log: which action was taken or approved, when, and by whom (a Shopify user ID, the schedule, a webhook or the system). Log details do not contain customer personal data.
  • Charge records: the price band that applied and the identifiers of the charges created through Shopify. Shopify handles payment. We do not receive or store payment details.

Data we process but do not store

  • Email addresses from Shopify orders are read in order to compute the hash described above. The address itself is not stored.
  • Email addresses of Klaviyo profiles are read from Klaviyo at the moment they are needed: when a batch of profiles is suppressed or restored, because Klaviyo's suppression interface works with email addresses, and when the merchant downloads the list of profiles in a simulation as a CSV file to review it before approving. They are not stored and not written to logs.
  • Klaviyo engagement data (such as emails received, opens, clicks, purchases, site activity, SMS consent and list membership) is read to evaluate the cleanup criteria. While a scan is running, the dates and the consent and suppression status read for each profile are held as working data, without the email address, and are deleted when the scan ends.

Data we do not collect

We do not store customer email addresses in readable form, customer names, postal addresses, phone numbers, payment details, order contents or the content of the merchant's emails.

Email from the app

On the effective date of this policy the app sends no email, and we do not store a merchant email address for that purpose. We plan to add operational notices for merchants: that a scheduled simulation is ready to approve, the result of a cleanup, and a monthly summary. When we do, they will go to the contact address of the store, they will be about the merchant's own use of the app and not marketing, and we will first update this policy and add the delivery provider to the list in section 6. The app never sends email to a merchant's customers.

4. Why we process it

  • To provide the service the merchant asked for: audit the list, apply the cleanup criteria, simulate, suppress the approved profiles, undo a run and schedule monthly cleanups.
  • To keep buyers from being suppressed, by matching Shopify orders to Klaviyo profiles through hashes.
  • To bill our flat monthly price through Shopify.
  • To keep a record of what was changed, by whom and when, so that changes are traceable and reversible.
  • To secure the service and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use customers' data to build profiles of our own.

Where a law such as the GDPR requires a legal basis for processing: for the personal data of a merchant's customers we act on the merchant's instructions, and the merchant is responsible for the legal basis. For the data we control, we rely on performing our agreement with the merchant, on our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the service, and on our legal obligations.

5. What the app changes in Klaviyo

The app changes the email suppression status of the profiles included in a simulation the merchant approved, and can remove that suppression again. It also creates and maintains one segment in the merchant's Klaviyo account, which it uses to find the profiles that meet the cleanup criteria. It does not delete profiles, it does not change consent or subscription status, and it does not change the merchant's Klaviyo plan or billing settings.

6. Who we share data with

We use one sub-processor to run TierSaver:

Sub-processorLocationWhat it does
Cloudflare, Inc.United StatesHosts the app and this website (Cloudflare Workers), the app's database (D1) and its background jobs (Workflows), and keeps operational logs

We do not use an email delivery provider or an error monitoring provider. We will update this list before we add any sub-processor.

Two other companies are involved, under their own agreements with the merchant:

  • Shopify is the platform through which the app is installed and used, and it bills and collects all charges. We do not receive or store payment details.
  • Klaviyo holds the merchant's profiles. The app exchanges data with the merchant's own Klaviyo account, using the access the merchant granted. Klaviyo processes that data under its agreement with the merchant, and is not our sub-processor.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to the data.

7. Where data is processed

We are a United States company. The app's database is stored with Cloudflare, with its primary region in eastern North America. Cloudflare runs the app and this website on its global network, so a request may be handled at a Cloudflare location close to the person making it.

If you are outside the United States, your data, and the data of your customers described in section 3, is transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection law may differ from the law where you are. If the law that applies to you requires a specific agreement for that transfer, write to hello@tiersaver.com.

8. Security

  • Access tokens are encrypted before storage (AES-GCM), and the key is kept separately from the database.
  • Customer email addresses are stored only as one-way SHA-256 hashes.
  • Data in transit is protected with TLS.
  • The app requests the minimum permissions it needs in Shopify and in Klaviyo.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting personal data, we will notify the affected merchants as the law requires.

9. How long we keep data

  • While the app is installed: the data described in section 3 is kept so the app can work and so runs can be undone.
  • On uninstall: the Shopify and Klaviyo access tokens are deleted immediately.
  • After uninstall: all remaining data stored for the store is deleted when Shopify sends its shop deletion request, which Shopify sends 48 hours after an app is uninstalled.
  • When a customer asks a store to erase their data: on Shopify's customer deletion request we delete that customer's hashed purchase record and remove their hash from past simulations and runs. Klaviyo profile IDs in the run history are kept so a run can still be undone.
  • When a customer asks a store for their data: the only data we hold that is derived from a customer is a one-way hash and the date of their last order. There is nothing readable to export.

Uninstalling the app does not restore suppressed profiles in Klaviyo, and does not delete anything in the merchant's Klaviyo or Shopify accounts.

We keep no charge records after that deletion. Invoices and charges remain in the merchant's Shopify account, under Shopify's own retention rules.

Operational logs kept by Cloudflare do not contain email addresses or access tokens. They are kept for the period set by Cloudflare's own log retention.

10. This website

tiersaver.com sets no cookies, has no forms, and uses no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts. Fonts and all other files are served from the same site, and pages make no requests to other sites. The list health check runs in your browser, and the numbers you enter are not sent to us.

Like any website, the hosting provider processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the service.

If you email us, we use your message and address to reply.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Merchants can exercise these rights by writing to hello@tiersaver.com. Customers of a store should contact the store first.

12. Children

TierSaver is a business tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If the change is significant, we will tell merchants in the app or by email before it takes effect.
