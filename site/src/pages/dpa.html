Annex II. Security measures

  • Data minimization. Customer email addresses are not stored in readable form. They are stored only as keyed, per-store pseudonymized hashes (HMAC-SHA-256, with a key derived for each store from a secret kept outside the database), so the hashes of one store cannot be matched with those of another. Names, postal addresses, phone numbers and order contents are not stored.
  • Encryption. Shopify and Klaviyo access tokens are encrypted before storage (AES-GCM). The encryption key and the hashing key are kept separately from the database. Data in transit is protected with TLS.
  • Least privilege. The app requests only the Shopify and Klaviyo permissions it uses. Access to the systems that hold customer data is limited to the people who operate TierSaver.
  • Separation between merchants. Every record is tied to one store, and the app serves a store only the records of that store.
  • Integrity of requests. Requests from Shopify are verified by their signature before they are processed. The Klaviyo connection uses OAuth with a one-time value bound to the merchant's browser.
  • Logs. The app does not write customer email addresses, names, access tokens or authorization codes to its logs. The hosting provider's per-request logs are switched off for the app.
  • Traceability. The app records who approved or started each cleanup and undo, and when.
  • Assistance. Customer data requests from Shopify are verified, recorded and listed in the app, where the merchant downloads the data held about that customer as a structured, machine-readable file. Customer deletion requests are carried out automatically.
  • Sub-processors. The sub-processor in Annex III is bound by a written contract to protect the data, to use it only to provide its service to us and to keep it secure and confidential.
  • Deletion. Working data is deleted on a schedule, tokens are deleted on uninstall, and all remaining data of a store is deleted after uninstall: on Shopify's shop deletion request, and by the app itself 10 days after the uninstall if that request has not arrived, as section 9 of the privacy policy describes.
