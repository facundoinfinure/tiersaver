In Shopify

PermissionWhat it is used for
read_ordersReading your orders, to find which email addresses have bought from your store. A profile matched to an order is kept active. TierSaver reads them every hour and before each audit. It stores a keyed hash of the address and the date of the last order, not the order itself.

TierSaver does not change anything in your store: it has no write permission in Shopify. With this permission alone Shopify only returns recent orders. As long as TierSaver cannot check a profile against enough of your order history, that profile is kept active.

Charges are created through Shopify's own billing, which you approve separately. See billing and charges.

In Klaviyo

You approve these on Klaviyo's own screen when you connect. Klaviyo names them as below.

PermissionWhat it is used for
accounts:readIdentifying which Klaviyo account is connected. Klaviyo requires it for every connected app.
profiles:readReading the profiles that meet the criteria and the members of the lists and segments you protect, and reading a profile's email address and suppression status right before it is suppressed or restored, or when it is included in your CSV download.
profiles:writeSuppressing profiles in a cleanup you approved. Klaviyo requires it, together with subscriptions:write, for bulk suppression. TierSaver does not edit or delete profiles.
subscriptions:writeSuppressing profiles in a cleanup, and removing that suppression when you restore one.
subscriptions:readChecking the status of the suppression jobs Klaviyo runs for a cleanup or a restore.
lists:readListing your lists so you can mark some as protected, and reading their members.
segments:readListing your segments so you can mark some as protected, and reading the members of TierSaver's own segment.
segments:writeCreating and maintaining the one segment described below. No other segment is created, changed or deleted.
metrics:readFinding the metrics in your account that record opens, clicks, purchases and site activity.
usage:readReading Klaviyo's own count of your active profiles and the date your billing period ends, which the monthly schedule can use as your renewal day. It only adds information: no cleanup and no charge depends on it.

What it does not ask for

The lists above are complete. TierSaver has no access to your campaigns, flows or templates, cannot send messages, and does not read your events.

The one segment it creates

The audit creates a segment in your Klaviyo account named "{{app.segmentName}}". It holds the criteria Klaviyo publishes for suppressing unengaged profiles, and Klaviyo itself evaluates it. Do not edit or delete it while you use the app: an audit cannot run on a segment that was changed, and the next audit restores it.

How to remove access

  • Klaviyo only: on the app's home page, under "Klaviyo connection", select "Disconnect Klaviyo". The app asks Klaviyo to revoke its access and deletes its stored tokens.
  • Everything: uninstall the app in Shopify. That also revokes the Klaviyo access.

In both cases suppressed profiles stay suppressed. See uninstalling and disconnecting.
