Your list size is the number of active profiles in your Klaviyo account: profiles with an email address that are not suppressed. Prices do not include taxes, which Shopify may add.

When a charge is created

  • Only when a cleanup suppresses profiles. If Klaviyo confirms that a cleanup suppressed nobody, it is not charged.
  • The full price for your list size. The charge is the same whether the cleanup suppressed a few profiles or many.
  • At most one charge every {{fee.minDays}} days, even if more than one cleanup runs in that time.
  • Never more than {{fee.maxPerWindow}} in a {{fee.windowDays}}-day period.
  • {{fee.holdHours}} hours after the cleanup finishes. The charge is created then, once Klaviyo has confirmed the suppressions. A cleanup that you restore in full within those {{fee.holdHours}} hours is not charged.
  • Restoring later does not refund. Restoring a cleanup after its charge was created, or restoring only part of it, does not refund the charge. See undo.
  • Lists above {{plans.maxProfiles}} active profiles are not charged automatically. There is no public price for them: write to {{contact.email}} and we will quote your list individually.

The app shows the price of a cleanup, or the reason it would not be charged, before you approve it.

What is free

  • The audit, as often as you like.
  • Simulations.
  • Any month in which no cleanup suppresses profiles. If you do not approve a cleanup, or a simulation finds nobody to suppress, there is no charge.

What the price does not depend on

The price depends on your list size. It does not grow with the number of profiles a cleanup suppresses, and it is not tied to your Klaviyo plan or invoice in any way.

How you are billed

All charges go through Shopify and appear on your Shopify invoice. TierSaver does not ask for a card. Before your first cleanup Shopify asks you to accept TierSaver's pricing; accepting does not charge anything by itself. The steps, and what each charge status means, are in billing and charges.
