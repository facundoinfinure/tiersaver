Your list size is the number of active profiles in your Klaviyo account: profiles with an email address that are not suppressed.

What is free

  • The audit, as often as you like.
  • Simulations.
  • Any month in which no cleanup runs. If you do not approve a cleanup, or a simulation finds nobody to suppress, there is no charge for that month.

What the price does not depend on

The price depends on your list size and on nothing else. It is not tied to how many profiles are suppressed, and it is not tied to your Klaviyo plan or invoice in any way.

How you are billed

All charges go through Shopify and appear on your Shopify invoice. TierSaver does not ask for a card. You approve the charge in Shopify before the first paid cleanup, and you can cancel by uninstalling the app.

If your list changes size

The price band is set by your active profile count. The app shows which price applies before each cleanup.
