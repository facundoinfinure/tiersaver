Your Klaviyo list

Profiles that were suppressed stay suppressed. TierSaver does not restore anyone when you uninstall. You can remove suppressions from Klaviyo at any time: Klaviyo lets you do it one profile at a time, or in bulk for a list or segment.

If a cleanup needs correcting, restore it before you uninstall. After uninstalling, the app no longer has access to your Klaviyo account and its cleanup log is deleted.

The monthly schedule stops. Nothing further is suppressed.

The segment TierSaver created in your Klaviyo account, "{{app.segmentName}}", is not removed by the app. You can delete it in Klaviyo once you no longer use TierSaver.

Your data

  • Immediately: TierSaver asks Klaviyo to revoke its access and deletes its access tokens for your Shopify store and your Klaviyo account. From that moment it cannot read or change anything.
  • When Shopify sends its shop deletion request: TierSaver deletes everything else it stored for your store, including settings, audits, simulations, cleanup logs and hashed purchase records. Shopify sends that request 48 hours after an app is uninstalled.
  • 10 days after you uninstall: if Shopify's request has not arrived by then, TierSaver deletes that data itself.

What is stored in the first place is listed in data and privacy.

Billing

Uninstalling cancels your TierSaver plan: Shopify stops charging it. The period you already paid for is not refunded. If you install the app again later, a new plan starts when you approve a cleanup. See billing and your plan.

Disconnecting Klaviyo without uninstalling

On the app's home page, under "Klaviyo connection", the "Disconnect Klaviyo" button removes TierSaver's access to your Klaviyo account: the app asks Klaviyo to revoke it and deletes the tokens it stored.

  • Suppressed profiles stay suppressed and nothing is deleted in Klaviyo.
  • Your settings and your cleanup history stay in the app.
  • Audits, cleanups, restores and the monthly schedule do not run while Klaviyo is disconnected.
  • To connect again, select "Connect Klaviyo" on the home page and approve access in Klaviyo.

If the Klaviyo connection is lost

If TierSaver's access to Klaviyo expires or is removed in Klaviyo while the app is still installed, the home page shows "Reconnect Klaviyo" and cleanups are paused until you connect again. Profiles already suppressed stay as they are.
