The History page

History lists your most recent cleanups and restores, newest first. Each row shows when it started, whether it is a cleanup or a restore, whether the monthly schedule started it, its status, and how many profiles were sent to Klaviyo out of the total. "Details" opens its page.

Statuses

StatusMeaning
QueuedIt has been started and is waiting to begin.
In progressProfiles are being sent to Klaviyo in batches. The page updates by itself; you can leave it.
CompletedEvery batch was sent to Klaviyo.
Completed with issuesIt finished, but some profiles were not processed because of an error. The counts show how many.
Did not runIt could not run. The page gives the reason.
CancelledIt was stopped before it finished.

If a cleanup or restore stopped early, a notice on its page gives the reason, and the profiles it did not reach were left untouched. The reasons are listed in troubleshooting.

The counts

  • In this cleanup. The profiles in the simulation you approved.
  • Sent to Klaviyo to be suppressed. What TierSaver submitted. Klaviyo then applies it in the background.
  • Left untouched. Profiles that were no longer in Klaviyo, had no email address, or whose address had changed since the simulation.
  • Not processed because of an error. Shown only when there are any.
  • Restored later. Shown when part or all of the cleanup was restored.

The charge

A cleanup's page states what happened with its charge: an estimated charge that has not been sent to Shopify yet, the amount charged and when, or the reason nothing was charged. See billing and charges.

Klaviyo jobs

Klaviyo processes each batch as a background job. "Check jobs in Klaviyo" asks Klaviyo what it reports for them: the status of each job, how many profiles it processed and how many Klaviyo skipped. For a large cleanup the jobs are checked a page at a time. A job that Klaviyo can no longer return is counted as one that could not be read.

The profile count Klaviyo shows in your account can take up to 72 hours to update after a cleanup.

Restore

The section "Undo this cleanup" has the Restore button and links to any restore already made from that cleanup. See undo for what Restore does and its limits.

How long history is kept

History stays while the app is installed. It is deleted with the rest of your store's data after you uninstall. See uninstalling and disconnecting.
