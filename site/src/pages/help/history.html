The History page

History lists your most recent cleanups and restores, newest first. Each row shows when it started, whether it is a cleanup or a restore, whether the monthly schedule started it, its status, and how many profiles were sent to Klaviyo out of the total. "Details" opens its page.

Statuses

StatusMeaning
QueuedIt has been started and is waiting to begin.
In progressProfiles are being sent to Klaviyo in batches. The page updates by itself; you can leave it.
CompletedEvery batch was sent to Klaviyo.
Completed with issuesIt finished, but some profiles were not processed because of an error. The counts show how many.
Did not runIt could not run. The page gives the reason.
CancelledIt was stopped before it finished. Profiles already sent stay suppressed and can be restored; the rest were not touched.

A cleanup or restore that stops responding is closed automatically, normally within one to two hours. Its page then shows what was really sent, so a cleanup closed this way can be restored, and you can run a new audit for the profiles it did not reach.

If a cleanup or restore stopped early, a notice on its page gives the reason, and the profiles it did not reach were left untouched. The reasons are listed in troubleshooting.

The counts

  • In this cleanup. The profiles in the simulation you approved. The number suppressed can be lower.
  • Sent to Klaviyo to be suppressed. What TierSaver submitted. Klaviyo then applies it in the background.
  • Left untouched. Profiles that were checked again right before sending and were no longer candidates, with the reason for each group: for example an order placed after the simulation, a changed email address, or a profile that is already suppressed or no longer exists in Klaviyo.
  • Sent, but Klaviyo's answer was lost. Shown only when there are any. Those profiles may have been suppressed, and a restore includes them.
  • Not processed because of an error. Shown only when there are any.
  • Restored later. Shown when part or all of the cleanup was restored.

Stopping a cleanup

While a cleanup is in progress its page has a "Stop cleanup" button. TierSaver then stops sending profiles to Klaviyo; the batch being sent at that moment, up to 100 profiles, still goes through. Profiles already sent stay suppressed, and you can restore them from the same page once the cleanup has closed.

Your plan

Cleanups are included in your monthly plan: there is no charge per cleanup, and restoring one does not refund the month. Your plan, its price and the date the current period ends are in Settings. See billing and your plan.

Klaviyo jobs

Klaviyo processes each batch as a background job. "Check jobs in Klaviyo" asks Klaviyo what it reports for them: the status of each job, how many profiles it processed and how many Klaviyo skipped. For a large cleanup the jobs are checked a page at a time. A job that Klaviyo can no longer return is counted as one that could not be read.

Klaviyo updates its count on its own schedule, so the profile count it shows in your account may not change right away.

Restore

The section "Restore this cleanup" has the Restore button and links to any restore already made from that cleanup. See undo for what Restore does and its limits.

How long history is kept

History stays while the app is installed. It is deleted with the rest of your store's data after you uninstall. See uninstalling and disconnecting.
