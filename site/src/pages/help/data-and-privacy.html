What TierSaver does not store

  • Customer email addresses in readable form.
  • Customer names, postal addresses or phone numbers.
  • The contents of orders: products, amounts or payment details.
  • The content of your emails or campaigns.

Email addresses pass through TierSaver's systems briefly in four moments: when an order's email is turned into a hash, when an audit turns the email of each profile it evaluates into a hash, when a batch of profiles is sent to Klaviyo to be suppressed or restored, and when you download the list of profiles in a simulation as a CSV file. They are not written to the database or to logs.

While an audit runs, TierSaver holds working data for each profile it evaluates: the profile ID, the hash of its email address, its dates and its consent and suppression status. It is deleted when the audit finishes or fails, and in any case about 24 hours after the audit started.

How hashes are used

A hash is a fingerprint of a normalized email address. TierSaver compares the fingerprint from a Shopify order with the fingerprint of a Klaviyo profile's email to know that a profile belongs to a buyer.

The fingerprints are keyed hashes (HMAC-SHA-256), computed with a secret key that is unique to your store and kept outside the database. They are keyed, per-store pseudonymized hashes, not anonymous data: they still relate to a person, so TierSaver treats hashes and Klaviyo profile IDs as personal data. Without the key, a fingerprint cannot be computed from an email address, and the fingerprints of your store cannot be matched with those of another store.

Access and permissions

TierSaver requests only the permissions it uses. In Shopify, it reads orders, and of the protected customer fields only the email address. In Klaviyo, it reads profiles with their dates and their consent and suppression status, it does not read individual opens or clicks, and the only change it makes to a profile is its suppression status, for the profiles in a cleanup you approved. It also creates and keeps one segment in your Klaviyo account, from the first audit, which Klaviyo uses to evaluate the cleanup criteria. The audit changes no profile.

Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. The encryption key is held separately from the database.

Where it runs

The app and its database run on Cloudflare, with the database in the United States (eastern North America). The person who operates and supports TierSaver works from Argentina and can access the data from there for maintenance and support. Charges are handled by Shopify. See section 7 of the privacy policy, and the Data Processing Addendum for the terms that cover transfers.

Deletion

  • When you uninstall: tokens are deleted as soon as Shopify notifies TierSaver, and TierSaver asks Klaviyo to revoke its access. Everything else is deleted when Shopify sends its shop deletion request, 48 hours after uninstalling, and as a backstop TierSaver purges the data of an uninstalled store within about 10 days. Deleted data can remain for up to 30 days in the hosting provider's point-in-time recovery copies of the database, which cannot be switched off, before it is permanently removed. See what happens on uninstall.
  • When a customer asks your store to erase their data: Shopify notifies TierSaver, which deletes that customer's hashed purchase record and removes their hash from past cleanups and simulations. Their Klaviyo profile ID stays in the cleanup history so the cleanup can still be undone.
  • When a customer asks for a copy of their data: TierSaver holds a keyed hash of their email address, the date of their last order and, if their profile was in a simulation or a cleanup, the Klaviyo profile ID, the reason, the status and the dates. When Shopify sends TierSaver the customer's data request, TierSaver records it and gives you that information in the app, so you can answer your customer.

Profiles in Klaviyo are never deleted by TierSaver. They are your data, in your Klaviyo account.

Cookies

The app sets one cookie, __Host-ts_klaviyo_oauth, while you connect your Klaviyo account. It protects that step against forged requests, contains no personal data and lasts at most 10 minutes.

This website

tiersaver.com sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts. The list health check runs entirely in your browser; the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
