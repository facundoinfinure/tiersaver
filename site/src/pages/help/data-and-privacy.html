What TierSaver does not store

  • Customer email addresses in readable form.
  • Customer names, postal addresses or phone numbers.
  • The contents of orders: products, amounts or payment details.
  • The content of your emails or campaigns.

Email addresses pass through TierSaver's systems briefly in four moments: when an order's email is turned into a hash, when an audit turns the email of each profile it evaluates into a hash, when a batch of profiles is sent to Klaviyo to be suppressed or restored, and when you download the list of profiles in a simulation as a CSV file. They are not written to the database or to logs.

While an audit runs, TierSaver holds working data for each profile it evaluates: the profile ID, the hash of its email address, its dates and its consent and suppression status. It is deleted when the audit finishes or fails, and in any case about 24 hours after the audit started.

How hashes are used

A hash is a fingerprint of a normalized email address. TierSaver compares the fingerprint from a Shopify order with the fingerprint of a Klaviyo profile's email to know that a profile belongs to a buyer. The stored fingerprint does not show the address, but it is pseudonymized data, not anonymous data: someone who already knows an email address can compute its fingerprint, so TierSaver treats hashes and Klaviyo profile IDs as personal data.

Access and permissions

TierSaver requests only the permissions it uses. In Shopify, it reads orders, and of the protected customer fields only the email address. In Klaviyo, it reads profiles with their dates and their consent and suppression status, it does not read individual opens or clicks, and the only change it makes to a profile is its suppression status, for the profiles in a cleanup you approved. It also keeps one segment in your Klaviyo account, which Klaviyo uses to evaluate the cleanup criteria.

Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. The encryption key is held separately from the database.

Where it runs

The app and its database run on Cloudflare. Charges are handled by Shopify.

Deletion

  • When you uninstall: tokens are deleted as soon as Shopify notifies TierSaver, and everything else when Shopify sends its shop deletion request, 48 hours after uninstalling. Deleted data can remain for a limited period in the hosting provider's encrypted backups before it is overwritten. See what happens on uninstall.
  • When a customer asks your store to erase their data: Shopify notifies TierSaver, which deletes that customer's hashed purchase record and removes their hash from past runs and simulations.
  • When a customer asks for a copy of their data: TierSaver holds a SHA-256 hash of their email address, the date of their last order and, if their profile was in a simulation or a cleanup, the Klaviyo profile ID, the reason, the status and the dates. Write to hello@tiersaver.com and TierSaver sends that information to you so you can answer your customer.

Profiles in Klaviyo are never deleted by TierSaver. They are your data, in your Klaviyo account.

Cookies

The app sets one cookie, __Host-ts_klaviyo_oauth, while you connect your Klaviyo account. It protects that step against forged requests, contains no personal data and lasts at most 10 minutes.

This website

tiersaver.com sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts. The list health check runs entirely in your browser; the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
