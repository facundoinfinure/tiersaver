When cleanups run

List hygiene works best as a routine. New profiles go quiet every month, and Klaviyo's bulk suppression applies only to the current members of a list or segment, so it does not repeat by itself.

After your first manual cleanup, TierSaver runs once a month. By default it runs {{schedule.leadDays}} days before your plan's renewal date, because Klaviyo says the profile count shown in billing may take up to 72 hours to refresh after a large cleanup. Dates follow your store's time zone.

You tell TierSaver your renewal day during setup. In Klaviyo it is shown under Billing, on the Overview tab.

Before each monthly cleanup you get a new simulation in the app, waiting for your approval for 24 hours. See simulation and approval.

What Klaviyo says about suppressed profiles and plans

These are Klaviyo's own statements, from its help center:

  • Suppressed profiles do not count toward your email plan's active profile count.
  • Your plan has to cover the number of active profiles you have at the end of your billing cycle.
  • A plan does not automatically move down just because you have fewer profiles. You can choose a smaller plan yourself after your count updates, or enable auto-downgrade based on profiles.
  • With auto-downgrade enabled, Klaviyo checks your active profile count shortly before your billing cycle renews, and an eligible change takes effect for the next cycle.

What TierSaver does and does not do

  • It does show you, as a reference, which Klaviyo plan size your active profile count corresponds to.
  • It does not change your Klaviyo plan, your billing settings or your auto-downgrade preference. Those are yours to decide, in Klaviyo.
  • It does not promise any effect on your Klaviyo plan or invoice.

If you want Klaviyo to adjust your plan size automatically

These are Klaviyo's steps as published on the date below. You need to be an Owner or Admin with billing permissions.

  1. Select your account name in the lower left, then go to Billing, then Preferences.
  2. If your Email upgrade preference is "Automatically upgrade", select "Flexible overages" or "None" and click Update. Klaviyo does not allow auto-downgrade together with auto-upgrade.
  3. In the "Profiles auto-downgrade" section, select "Auto-downgrade profiles" and click that section's Update button.

Changing your upgrade preference affects what happens when you reach your email sending limit. Read Klaviyo's article before you change it, and choose what suits how you send.

Klaviyo also notes that a plan change takes effect in the next billing cycle, that the smaller plan must cover your active profile count, and that changing plans does not change your renewal date.
