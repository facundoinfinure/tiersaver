How to restore a cleanup

  1. Open TierSaver and go to History.
  2. Open the cleanup you want to correct.
  3. In the section "Restore this cleanup", select the Restore button.

Restore becomes available once the cleanup has finished and Klaviyo has finished applying it. If you try too early, the app asks you to try again in a minute or two. The restore then appears in History as its own entry.

What Restore does

  • It removes the manual suppression that cleanup added, so those profiles can receive marketing email again.
  • It includes profiles that were sent to Klaviyo without a confirmed answer, because they may have been suppressed.
  • It does not change consent. Profiles that unsubscribed, bounced or have an invalid address stay unsendable in Klaviyo.
  • It does not touch profiles from other cleanups, or profiles that were never part of a TierSaver cleanup.

The limits of Restore

  • It needs the app installed and connected to Klaviyo. After you uninstall, remove suppressions in Klaviyo instead.
  • It may not tell your own suppressions apart. Klaviyo records only one kind of manual suppression and does not say who added it. Restore leaves a profile untouched when Klaviyo's dates show that it was already suppressed before the cleanup. Otherwise it removes the suppression the cleanup added, and it may not be able to tell that one apart from a suppression you added yourself to the same profile. Check before you restore.
  • Customers erased on request are not restored. When a customer asks your store to erase their data, TierSaver removes the link between past cleanups and that customer's Klaviyo profile, so Restore leaves the profile as it is. You can remove its suppression in Klaviyo.
  • Emails are not sent later. Whatever those profiles did not receive while they were suppressed is not sent to them afterwards.
  • Your plan. Restoring a cleanup, in full or in part, does not refund the month and does not change your TierSaver plan. See how pricing works.
  • Some profiles may be left as they are. Each profile is checked again right before the restore, and one that no longer exists in Klaviyo or has no email address cannot be restored. The restore's page shows how many were left untouched and why.

What happens to restored profiles

  • They count as active profiles again. Klaviyo updates its count on its own schedule, so the count it shows may not change right away.
  • TierSaver leaves them alone for {{criteria.undoLockDays}} days. A restored profile is left out of every cleanup during that time. If you restore a cleanup, you are telling TierSaver those profiles should stay.
  • Klaviyo has its own waiting period. Its help center says that once a profile is suppressed and then unsuppressed, it cannot be manually suppressed again until {{klaviyo.resuppressDays}} days after the previous suppression. The rule is not active during the first 30 days after a new Klaviyo account is created.

What undo is not for

Undo is not a way to suppress profiles for a while and bring them back later. Suppression is a decision to stop emailing someone. If you expect to email a group again, protect it instead: mark the list or segment as protected in Settings and it will not be selected.

If your Klaviyo plan has renewed, or may have renewed, since the cleanup, the app asks you to confirm that you are restoring the profiles to correct a mistake. Suppressing profiles before a renewal and restoring them afterwards is against TierSaver's terms.

The cleanup log

Each cleanup is recorded: when it started and finished, whether you started it or the schedule did, who approved it, the criteria in effect and how many profiles were suppressed. For each profile the log keeps the Klaviyo profile ID, a keyed hash of its email address and the reason it was selected. That record is what Restore works from. See History.
