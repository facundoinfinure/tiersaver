What the audit shows

  • Active profiles. Profiles with an email address that are not suppressed from email.
  • Profiles that meet the cleanup criteria. Active profiles that have never engaged, under the default criteria.
  • Profiles that would stay active. Everyone else, with the reason each group is kept: bought, opened, clicked, visited, too new, SMS subscriber, in a protected list, or incomplete data.

Next to each figure the app explains how it was calculated.

As a secondary reference, the audit also shows which Klaviyo plan size your active profile count corresponds to, now and after a cleanup, using Klaviyo's published plan sizes. See the monthly schedule and your Klaviyo plan.

The audit changes nothing

The audit only reads. It does not suppress, tag or edit any profile, and it does not change anything in your Klaviyo account. Suppression only happens later, after you approve a simulation.

Where the numbers come from

  • Engagement (emails received, opens, clicks, site activity, checkouts) comes from your Klaviyo account.
  • Purchases come from two places: your Shopify orders and Klaviyo's own purchase data. A purchase in either one keeps the profile.
  • SMS consent and list membership come from Klaviyo.

If TierSaver cannot see enough history to be sure about a profile, the profile is counted as staying active. On a newly connected store this can mean the audit finds few or no candidates at first. That is intended.

What you need

A Shopify store with the app installed, and a Klaviyo account you can authorize. You connect Klaviyo by signing in to Klaviyo and approving access; TierSaver never asks you to paste a password.
