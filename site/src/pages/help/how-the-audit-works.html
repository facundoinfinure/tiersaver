What the audit shows

  • Active profiles. The profiles Klaviyo counts toward your plan: profiles with an email address that are not suppressed from email.
  • Profiles that stopped engaging. Active profiles that none of the protection rules cover, using the default settings.
  • Your current tier. The profile limit of your Klaviyo plan, read from Klaviyo's billing usage data.
  • The tier a clean list would fit. The tier your list would fall into if those profiles were suppressed.
  • Estimated monthly saving. The difference between the list prices of the two tiers, in US dollars.

Next to each figure the app explains how it was calculated.

The audit changes nothing

The audit only reads. It does not suppress, tag or edit any profile, and it does not change your Klaviyo plan. Suppression only happens later, after you approve a simulation.

Why the saving is an estimate

TierSaver uses Klaviyo's published list prices, as read on {{prices.capturedOn}}. Klaviyo does not publish prices through its API, so the app keeps a dated copy of the price table and tells you which copy it used.

Your invoice can differ from list price. For example, some accounts pay a discounted rate. The audit asks what you pay today; if you tell it, estimates and fees are limited by that amount.

A saving stops being an estimate when Klaviyo's billing data shows your account on a lower tier after your billing cycle renews. See how the fee is calculated.

When the audit cannot estimate a saving

  • Free plan. There is no bill to lower, so there is nothing to estimate.
  • More than {{prices.maxProfiles}} active profiles. Klaviyo quotes custom pricing above that size, so there is no list price to compare.
  • A plan limit that is not in the price table. If your plan's profile limit does not match a published tier, TierSaver does not guess. It shows the profile counts and no dollar figure.
  • No tier change. If suppressing the profiles found would leave you in the same tier, the estimated saving is $0 and there would be no fee.

What you need

A Shopify store with the app installed, and a Klaviyo account you can authorize. You connect Klaviyo by signing in to Klaviyo and approving access; TierSaver never asks you to paste a password.
