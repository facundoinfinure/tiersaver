What the audit shows

  • Active profiles. Profiles with an email address that are not suppressed from email. The app says whether the figure is Klaviyo's own count or was counted by TierSaver.
  • Profiles that meet the criteria Klaviyo publishes for suppressing unengaged profiles: never opened, clicked, visited or bought. See the cleanup criteria.
  • Profiles a cleanup would suppress, and how many would stay active afterwards.
  • Profiles kept active even though they meet the criteria, with the reason: for example a purchase in your Shopify store, SMS consent, a list or segment you protect, or incomplete data. A profile can be kept for more than one reason, so those rows can overlap.

The app also shows TierSaver's monthly price for a list of your size. It does not show your Klaviyo plan or what you pay Klaviyo.

The audit changes no profile

The audit does not suppress, tag or edit any profile. It makes one change in your Klaviyo account: it creates a segment named "{{app.segmentName}}", which Klaviyo uses to evaluate the cleanup criteria. Do not edit or delete that segment while you use the app; if it is edited, the next audit restores it. Suppression only happens later, after you approve a simulation.

Where the numbers come from

  • Engagement (emails received, opens, clicks, site activity, checkouts) comes from your Klaviyo account.
  • Purchases come from two places: your Shopify orders and Klaviyo's own purchase data. A purchase in either one keeps the profile.
  • SMS consent and list membership come from Klaviyo.

If TierSaver cannot see enough history to be sure about a profile, the profile is counted as staying active. On a newly connected store this can mean the audit finds few or no candidates at first. That is intended. Troubleshooting lists the messages the app shows in that case.

Running it

On the app's home page, select "Run the free audit". Klaviyo works through your list in the background and the page shows how many profiles have been read so far; you can leave the page and come back. You can run the audit again as often as you like.

Every audit that finishes produces a simulation, which is what you review and approve before a cleanup.

What you need

A Shopify store with the app installed, and a Klaviyo account you can authorize. You connect Klaviyo by signing in to Klaviyo and approving access; TierSaver never asks you to paste a password or an API key. See getting started and the permissions TierSaver asks for.
