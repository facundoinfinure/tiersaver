What suppression does

According to Klaviyo's help center, when a profile is suppressed:

  • it remains in your account, but is no longer considered active for email;
  • it cannot receive marketing emails, and Klaviyo skips it at send time;
  • it does not count toward your email plan's active profile count;
  • its ability to receive SMS messages and push notifications is not affected.

A manual suppression does not change the profile's consent status. Klaviyo's example: a profile that was Subscribed remains Subscribed, but cannot receive email marketing while it is suppressed.

A suppressed profile stays suppressed even if that person later starts a checkout or buys. That is why TierSaver's protection rules are conservative, and why it can reactivate suppressed profiles that buy again if you switch that option on.

Unsubscribing is a different thing. It is the recipient's own choice about consent. Suppressing a profile does not change its consent status, and removing a suppression does not resubscribe anyone. TierSaver never changes consent.

Klaviyo also suppresses profiles on its own in some cases, for example after a hard bounce. TierSaver does not touch those.

Klaviyo's own guidance is that you should generally aim to suppress profiles instead of deleting them.

What reversing a suppression does and does not do

Removing a manual suppression makes the profile active again. It does not change consent: Klaviyo notes that a profile that had unsubscribed still cannot receive marketing emails after it is unsuppressed, and that suppressions caused by bounces are not removed this way.

There is also a waiting period before the same profile can be suppressed again. See undo and the 90-day rule.

What TierSaver adds

You can suppress by hand in Klaviyo, and Klaviyo recommends doing so for long-inactive profiles. A bulk suppression applies to the current members of a list or segment; it does not repeat on its own. TierSaver checks every candidate against your Shopify orders, shows a simulation first, schedules the cleanup ahead of your billing date and keeps a record of each run so it can be undone.
