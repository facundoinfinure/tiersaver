What suppression does

According to Klaviyo's help center, when a profile is suppressed:

  • it remains in your account, but is no longer considered active for email;
  • it stops receiving marketing email, campaigns and most flow emails, even if it had given consent: Klaviyo skips it at send time;
  • flow emails that Klaviyo has approved as transactional can still be sent to it;
  • its ability to receive SMS messages and push notifications is not affected;
  • it does not count toward your email plan's active profile count. On Klaviyo's Advanced KDP plan, suppressed profiles can still count toward billing.

A manual suppression does not change the profile's consent status. Klaviyo's example: a profile that was Subscribed remains Subscribed, but cannot receive email marketing while it is suppressed.

A suppressed profile stays suppressed even if that person later starts a checkout or buys. It is only unsuppressed if the person subscribes to your email again, or if you remove the suppression. Treat suppression as a decision to stop emailing someone, not as a pause. That is why TierSaver's default criteria only select profiles with no recorded engagement.

What this means for your campaigns and flows is in what changes after a cleanup.

Klaviyo's own guidance is that you should generally aim to suppress profiles instead of deleting them.

Unsubscribing is something else

Unsubscribing is the recipient's own choice. Klaviyo records it as an Unsubscribed consent status, not as a suppression reason. Suppressing a profile does not change its consent, and removing a suppression does not resubscribe anyone. TierSaver never changes consent.

A suppression is also not an unsubscribe record. If someone asks you to stop emailing them, handle that request in Klaviyo as an unsubscribe; do not rely on a TierSaver cleanup for it.

Klaviyo also suppresses profiles on its own in some cases, for example after a hard bounce or repeated soft bounces. TierSaver does not touch those.

There is a longer comparison in the guide Suppress, delete or unsubscribe.

Removing a suppression

Removing a manual suppression makes the profile active again. It does not change consent: Klaviyo notes that a profile that had unsubscribed still cannot receive marketing emails after it is unsuppressed, and that suppressions caused by bounces are not removed this way. After that, Klaviyo makes the profile wait before it can be suppressed again. See undo.
