Campaigns and flows

  • Suppressed profiles stop receiving your marketing email: campaigns and most flow emails. Klaviyo skips them at send time.
  • Flow emails that Klaviyo has approved as transactional still send to them.
  • This stays true if the person later buys or starts a checkout.

SMS and push

Suppression is for email only. A profile's ability to receive SMS messages and push notifications does not change. TierSaver also keeps any profile with SMS consent out of cleanups in the first place.

If the person subscribes again

A person who subscribes to your email again is unsuppressed by Klaviyo and can receive marketing email again. TierSaver does not unsuppress anyone by itself; the only way it removes a suppression is when you restore a cleanup.

Your active profile count

  • Suppressed profiles do not count toward your Klaviyo email plan's active profile count. Klaviyo's count can take up to 72 hours to update after a cleanup.
  • On Klaviyo's Advanced KDP plan, suppressed profiles can still count toward billing.
  • Your Klaviyo plan does not change automatically unless you enabled auto-downgrade in Klaviyo. TierSaver makes no promise about your Klaviyo plan or invoice. See the monthly schedule and your Klaviyo plan.

What stays the same

  • The profiles and their full history stay in your Klaviyo account. Nothing is deleted.
  • Consent does not change. A suppression is not an unsubscribe.

The message "Profiles from an earlier cleanup are active again"

The app shows this on the home page when an audit finds profiles that a TierSaver cleanup suppressed and that have since been unsuppressed in Klaviyo outside TierSaver. When that happens:

  • those profiles are kept active, and TierSaver does not suppress them again for {{criteria.undoLockDays}} days;
  • your monthly schedule is paused. Turn it back on in Settings once you have checked what happened.
