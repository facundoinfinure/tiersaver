Before the plan starts

Installing, connecting Klaviyo, the audit and the simulation are free and need no plan. In Settings, the Plan section says "No plan yet. It starts when you approve your first cleanup."

Starting the plan

The "Review cleanup" page states the monthly price for your list size and the plan's terms before you approve anything. When you approve your first cleanup, the app sends you to Shopify's approval screen for that plan, named "TierSaver list hygiene" with the list size it covers. The plan starts when you approve it there. If you decline, no plan starts and no cleanup can start; you can still use the audit and simulations.

The prices are in how pricing works.

Renewal

Shopify charges the plan every {{plan.intervalDays}} days, whether or not a cleanup runs that month, until you cancel. The charge appears on your Shopify invoice. Settings shows your plan, its price and the date the current period ends.

There is no charge per cleanup. Any cleanup you run while the plan is active, manual or automatic, is included.

Cleanups need an active plan

Right before a cleanup starts, TierSaver asks Shopify whether your store has an active plan that covers the list size shown in the simulation. If it does not, the cleanup does not start and nothing changes in Klaviyo.

When your list changes size

  • Your list grows past your plan. The next cleanup waits until you approve the new monthly price in Shopify, which you do when you approve that cleanup. Automatic mode waits for you too and never charges more without your approval. Shopify prorates the change. Until then you keep paying your current price.
  • Your list shrinks into a smaller plan. The app tells you, in Settings and on the "Review cleanup" page, and offers to switch. The change is not automatic. If you switch, Shopify asks you to approve the new price and prorates the change.

Restoring and refunds

Restoring a cleanup does not refund the month and does not change your plan. See undo.

Cancelling

Open Settings, go to the Plan section and select "Cancel plan". Uninstalling the app also cancels the plan. See uninstalling and disconnecting.

  • Shopify stops charging the plan. The period you already paid for is not refunded.
  • No cleanup can start, and automatic mode is switched off.
  • Audits stay free, and scheduled audits keep running.
  • Profiles already suppressed stay suppressed in Klaviyo, and you can still restore them from History.
  • A new plan starts if you approve another cleanup.

A plan on hold

If Shopify freezes the plan because of a payment problem with your store, cleanups stop until Shopify makes the plan active again. Settings then shows "Your plan is on hold in Shopify." The hold is resolved in your Shopify billing, not in TierSaver.

Development stores

On a development store the plan is a test plan: Shopify does not bill it.
