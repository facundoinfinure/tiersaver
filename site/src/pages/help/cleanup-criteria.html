The default criteria

A profile is a candidate only if all of these are true:

  • It was created at least {{criteria.ageDays}} days ago.
  • It has received at least {{criteria.emails}} emails in roughly the last {{criteria.weeks}} weeks.
  • It has never opened an email.
  • It has never clicked an email.
  • It has never visited your site or started a checkout.
  • It has never purchased, checked against both your Shopify orders and Klaviyo.

These follow the criteria Klaviyo publishes for its own suppression recommendations: created at least 365 days ago, 5 or more emails received over roughly the last 72 weeks, never opened or clicked, and no recorded site visits, product views, started checkouts or purchases.

Who is always kept

  • Buyers. Anyone with a purchase in your Shopify orders or in Klaviyo.
  • SMS subscribers. Any profile with SMS consent.
  • Protected lists and segments. Any list or segment you mark as protected, for example VIPs or wholesale accounts.
  • Profiles with incomplete data. If TierSaver cannot see a profile's creation date, or cannot see far enough back in your order or engagement history to be sure, the profile stays.
  • Profiles restored by an undo. They are left out of cleanups for {{criteria.undoLockDays}} days. See undo.

Profiles that are already suppressed, and profiles with no email address, are skipped entirely.

Buyers are checked twice

Klaviyo only knows about the purchases it has recorded. Your Shopify order history is an independent check. To match orders to Klaviyo profiles, TierSaver compares one-way hashes of email addresses, so it does not need to store your customers' email addresses. See data and privacy.

About opens

Klaviyo flags opens that may have been triggered by Apple's Mail Privacy Protection instead of a person. TierSaver counts those opens as engagement, which is the cautious choice: a flagged open keeps the profile.

The wider mode

You can opt in to a wider mode that uses time windows instead of "never". It comes with a warning in the app, because it can select people who engaged with you in the past. The windows cannot be set shorter than these floors:

SignalShortest window allowed
No opens, clicks or site activity{{criteria.widerEngagementDays}} days
No purchases{{criteria.widerPurchaseDays}} days
Profile age{{criteria.widerAgeDays}} days

SMS subscribers, protected lists and profiles with incomplete data are kept in the wider mode too. Changing the criteria discards any simulation that has not run yet: you get a new one to review.

Why the criteria are strict

Klaviyo's help center points out that a suppressed profile stays suppressed even if that person later starts a checkout or buys, and that suppressing too early can cost revenue. Suppression is meant for profiles that are truly inactive, so that is all the default mode selects.
