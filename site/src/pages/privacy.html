2. The roles involved

A merchant who installs the app decides which data is processed and why. For the personal data of the merchant's customers, the merchant is the controller (or "business") and we act as a processor (or "service provider") on the merchant's instructions, under our terms of service and our Data Processing Addendum. If you are a customer of a store that uses TierSaver and want to exercise your privacy rights, contact that store. We assist the store in responding.

3. What the app processes

Data we store

  • Store details: the store's myshopify.com domain, installation status and dates, the Shopify permissions granted, the store's time zone, and how far the store's orders have been read (from which date, up to which moment, and the position of a read in progress).
  • Access credentials: the Shopify access and refresh tokens and the Klaviyo OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored encrypted, with the permissions granted and the Klaviyo account ID.
  • Settings: cleanup criteria, protected lists or segments (by Klaviyo ID), the plan renewal day, run mode and the Klaviyo plan type.
  • Audit and simulation results: profile counts, the active-profile usage figures and billing period dates reported by Klaviyo, the Klaviyo plan size shown as a reference, estimates, and the criteria used.
  • Profiles selected for suppression: for each simulation and each cleanup, the Klaviyo profile ID, the reason the profile was selected, its processing status, the identifier of the Klaviyo job that processed it and, for matching purposes, a keyed hash of the profile's email address (described below). The per-profile list of a simulation that was never run is deleted after 7 days. Its counts are kept.
  • Hashed purchase records: for each buyer found in the store's Shopify orders, a keyed hash of the normalized order email address, the date of the most recent order, and a hash of the Shopify customer ID made with the same per-store key.
  • Activity log: which action was taken or approved, when, and by whom (the Shopify user ID of the staff member, the schedule, a webhook or the system). The same user ID is recorded on the audits, simulations and cleanups that person started or approved. Each manual approval records the version of the terms of service that was accepted, and so does turning automatic mode on, together with the price shown at that moment. We do not store staff names or email addresses. Log details do not contain customer personal data.
  • Privacy requests: when Shopify sends us a customer data request for the store, a record of the request (Shopify's request ID, the Shopify customer ID, the keyed hash of the customer's email address and the dates involved), so the merchant can download from the app's Settings, under "Customer data requests", the data we hold about that customer. The record is deleted after 90 days, or earlier if Shopify sends a deletion request for the same customer.
  • Klaviyo connection attempts: a short-lived marker for each attempt to connect a Klaviyo account (a hash of a one-time value, the store and the result). It contains no token and is deleted about 24 hours later.
  • Charge records: the list size and price band that applied, the number of profiles suppressed, the amount, whether a charge was created and why not, and the identifiers of the charges created through Shopify. Shopify handles payment. We do not receive or store payment details.
  • Record of an uncharged cleanup: only if a store uninstalls the app while a cleanup that was not restored in full is still waiting for its charge, one record for the Klaviyo account involved: a keyed hash of the Klaviyo account ID, the date of the cleanup and the amount that was not charged. It contains no store name, no staff data and no customer data. Sections 4 and 9 explain why we keep it and for how long.

How email addresses are hashed

Where this policy says "keyed hash", it means an HMAC-SHA-256 value computed with a key that is unique to each store. That key is derived from a secret kept outside the database. These are keyed, per-store pseudonymized hashes. They are pseudonymized data, not anonymous data: they still relate to a person, and we treat them, and the Klaviyo profile IDs we store, as personal data. Without the key, a hash cannot be computed from an email address, and the hashes of one store cannot be matched with those of another. The hash of a Klaviyo account ID in the record of an uncharged cleanup is made the same way, with one key for the whole service instead of one per store, so that the same Klaviyo account is recognized if it is connected again from another store.

Data we process but do not store

  • Shopify orders. The app reads the store's orders (its full order history where Shopify has granted that access), every hour and again right before each audit while the app is installed, and uses only the order's email address, date and customer ID to compute the hashed purchase record described above. The email address itself is not stored. Order notifications sent by Shopify can contain other order details. The app does not use or store them.
  • Email addresses of Klaviyo profiles are read from Klaviyo in three situations: during each audit, to compute the hash described above for the profiles being evaluated (and, when Klaviyo's own profile count is unavailable, to count the profiles that have an email address); when a batch of profiles is checked again and then suppressed or restored, because Klaviyo's suppression interface works with email addresses; and when the merchant downloads the list of profiles in a simulation as a CSV file to review it before approving. In each case the address is used in memory for that operation. It is not written to our database or to our logs.
  • Klaviyo profile and account data. Klaviyo evaluates the engagement criteria (emails received, opens, clicks, site activity, purchases) inside the segment the app maintains. The app does not read individual opens, clicks or other events. From Klaviyo the app reads: for each profile being evaluated, its ID, email address, creation date, date of last activity, and email and SMS consent and suppression status; the IDs of the members of that segment and of the lists or segments the merchant protected; the names of the account's lists, segments and metrics; and the account's ID, organization name, time zone, whether it is a test account, and its active-profile usage figures. Right before a batch of profiles is suppressed or restored, the app also reads each profile's current email address and its email suppressions, with their reason and date, to leave untouched anyone who is no longer a candidate. If that account request fails while the merchant is connecting, the app asks Klaviyo for the account record without limiting its fields; it uses only the fields listed here.
  • Working data of an audit. While an audit is running, we hold working data for each profile being evaluated: its Klaviyo profile ID, the hash of its email address, the dates it was created and last active, and its email and SMS consent and suppression status. It does not include the email address. It is deleted when the audit finishes or fails, and in any case about 24 hours after the audit started.

Data we do not collect

We do not store customer email addresses in readable form, customer names, postal addresses, phone numbers, payment details, order contents or the content of the merchant's emails.

Cookies in the app

The app sets one cookie, __Host-ts_klaviyo_oauth, when you start connecting your Klaviyo account. It ties Klaviyo's authorization response to your browser to prevent forged requests, contains no personal data, lasts at most 10 minutes and is removed when the connection step ends. It is strictly necessary and is not used for analytics or advertising. The app runs inside the Shopify admin and loads Shopify's own scripts. Shopify's cookies are governed by Shopify's policies.

Email from the app

On the effective date of this policy the app sends no email, and we do not store a merchant email address for that purpose. Notices are shown in the app. If we add operational notices by email in the future (for example, that a scheduled simulation is ready to approve, or the result of a cleanup), they will go to the contact address of the store, they will be about the merchant's own use of the app and not marketing, and we will first update this policy and add the delivery provider to the list in section 6. The app never sends email to a merchant's customers.

4. Why we process it

  • To provide the service the merchant asked for: audit the list, apply the cleanup criteria, simulate, suppress the approved profiles, undo a cleanup and schedule monthly cleanups.
  • To keep buyers from being suppressed, by matching Shopify orders to Klaviyo profiles through hashes.
  • To bill our flat monthly price through Shopify.
  • To keep a record of what was changed, by whom and when, so that changes are traceable and reversible.
  • To secure the service and to meet legal obligations.
  • To prevent misuse of the service: to make sure a Klaviyo account is connected to only one installed store at a time, and to keep the 72 hours in which a cleanup can be undone at no charge from being used to run cleanups without ever paying for them.

We do not sell or share personal data (as those terms are used in California privacy law), we do not use it for advertising, we do not combine one merchant's customer data with data from any other source, and we do not use customers' data to build profiles of our own.

Where a law such as the GDPR requires a legal basis for processing: for the personal data of a merchant's customers we act on the merchant's instructions, and the merchant is responsible for the legal basis. For the data we control, we rely on performing our agreement with the merchant, on our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the service, and on our legal obligations. For the record of an uncharged cleanup we rely on our legitimate interest in preventing misuse of the 72 hours without a charge. We kept that record as small as we could: it does not name the store, it holds a hash in place of the account ID, and it expires. You can object to it by writing to hello@tiersaver.com.

Automated processing

The app applies fixed rules chosen by the merchant, the cleanup criteria, to select profiles. The merchant reviews and approves the result, or authorizes it in advance in automatic mode. Cleanups, including automatic ones, only stop marketing email to a profile. They have no legal effect on a person and, in our assessment, do not affect a person in a similarly significant way. A customer who wants to receive the store's email again can resubscribe, and the store can restore any profile.

The app does not use machine learning or AI models to select profiles, and we do not build profiles of individuals for our own purposes. Each merchant is responsible for assessing this processing under the laws that apply to it.

5. What the app changes in Klaviyo

The app changes the email suppression status of the profiles included in a simulation the merchant approved, and can remove that suppression again. It also creates and maintains one segment in the merchant's Klaviyo account, which it uses to find the profiles that meet the cleanup criteria; this happens from the first audit, and the audit changes no profile. The app does not delete profiles, it does not change consent or subscription status, and it does not change the merchant's Klaviyo plan or billing settings.

Klaviyo records only one kind of manual suppression. When the merchant undoes a cleanup, the app asks Klaviyo to remove the manual suppression that the cleanup added. It leaves untouched a profile that Klaviyo shows as suppressed since before the cleanup, and the profile of a customer whose data was erased on request. Otherwise it may not be able to tell the cleanup's suppression apart from one that the merchant added to the same profile for its own reason, and may remove that one too. Profiles that unsubscribed, bounced or have an invalid address are not changed by an undo.

8. Security

  • Access tokens are encrypted before storage (AES-GCM), and the key is kept separately from the database.
  • Customer email addresses are stored only as keyed, per-store pseudonymized hashes, never in readable form. The hashing key is kept separately from the database.
  • Data in transit is protected with TLS.
  • The app requests only the permissions it uses in Shopify and in Klaviyo.
  • Access to the systems that hold this data is limited to the people who operate TierSaver.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting personal data, we will notify the affected merchants without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after we confirm it, with the information they need to meet their own obligations.

9. How long we keep data

  • While the app is installed: the data described in section 3 is kept so the app can work and so cleanups can be undone, except for the working data of an audit and the per-profile lists of simulations that were never run, which are deleted on the schedule given in section 3. Hashed purchase records are kept for as long as the app is installed, because the default criteria protect anyone who has ever purchased.
  • On uninstall: the Shopify and Klaviyo access tokens are deleted from our database as soon as Shopify notifies us, and we ask Klaviyo to revoke our access. You can also remove TierSaver from your Klaviyo account's integrations at any time.
  • After uninstall: all remaining data stored for the store, with the one exception described in the next item, is deleted from our database when Shopify sends its shop deletion request, which Shopify sends 48 hours after an app is uninstalled. As a backstop, if that request has not arrived, the app itself deletes the data of an uninstalled store 10 days after the uninstall. You can ask us to delete it sooner by writing to hello@tiersaver.com.
  • The one record that outlasts an uninstall: if a store uninstalls the app during the 72 hours before a cleanup's charge is created, and that cleanup was not restored in full, we keep the record of an uncharged cleanup described in section 3: a keyed hash of the Klaviyo account ID, the date of the cleanup and the amount that was not charged. It is not tied to the store, it contains no customer data, and Shopify's shop deletion request does not remove it. We use it only so that the next cleanup on the same Klaviyo account is charged as soon as Klaviyo confirms it, as section 6 of the terms describes. It is deleted as soon as a charge for that Klaviyo account has been created in Shopify, and in any case 400 days (about 13 months) after it was written.
  • When a customer asks a store to erase their data: on Shopify's customer deletion request we delete that customer's hashed purchase record, their rows in simulations and any record of a data request for them, and in past cleanups we remove their hash and replace their Klaviyo profile ID with a random value. The cleanup keeps its counts, but its history no longer points to that customer's profile, so a later undo does not restore it.
  • When a customer asks a store for their data: what we hold about a customer is limited to a keyed hash of their email address, the date of their most recent order, and, if their Klaviyo profile was included in a simulation or a cleanup, the Klaviyo profile ID, the reason it was selected, its processing status and the dates involved. When Shopify sends us a customer data request for a store, we record it and list it in the app's Settings, where the merchant can download the data we hold about that customer for 90 days and answer its customer within the time the law sets. The app does not send that data to the customer.

Uninstalling the app does not restore suppressed profiles in Klaviyo, and does not delete anything in the merchant's Klaviyo or Shopify accounts. The segment the app created in the merchant's Klaviyo account also stays there. The merchant can delete it in Klaviyo.

Deleted data can remain for up to 30 days in our hosting provider's point-in-time recovery copies of the database ("Time Travel"), which we cannot switch off, before it is permanently removed. We do not restore deleted data from those copies except to recover from an incident, and if we do, we delete it again.

Apart from the record of an uncharged cleanup described above, we keep no charge records after that deletion. Invoices and charges remain in the merchant's Shopify account, under Shopify's own retention rules.

Logs. The hosting provider's per-request logs are switched off for the app. The app writes its own operational logs, such as the type of event, counts and error codes. The app does not write customer email addresses, names, access tokens or authorization codes to them. Our hosting provider keeps those logs, and the working state of background jobs (identifiers, counters and cursors), for a limited period that it sets. Like any hosting provider, it also processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service.
