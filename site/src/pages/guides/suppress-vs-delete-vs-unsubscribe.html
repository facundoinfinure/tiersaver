Suppress

A suppressed profile stays in your account but stops receiving marketing email, campaigns and most flow emails, even if the person gave consent. Klaviyo skips it at send time. Flow emails that Klaviyo has approved as transactional can still be sent, and SMS and push eligibility are not affected.

Profiles become suppressed in several ways, according to Klaviyo:

  • you suppress them manually, one at a time, in bulk for a list or segment, or by uploading a file;
  • an email hard bounced;
  • an email soft bounced 7 consecutive times;
  • the suppression was carried over from a previous email provider.

A manual suppression is removed when the profile resubscribes to email marketing, or when you remove it. Removing it does not change consent and does not clear a bounce suppression. Once removed, Klaviyo does not let the same profile be manually suppressed again until {{klaviyo.resuppressDays}} days after the previous suppression.

Use it when you no longer want to email someone but want to keep their data for analysis and a complete picture of your customers. This is Klaviyo's general guidance.

Delete

Deleting removes the profile from your account. You can no longer open it or see its activity, and Klaviyo states that the action cannot be undone. If the same person engages with your site again, for example through a sign-up form, they enter your account as a new profile.

Use it when you are certain you no longer need the data, such as test profiles or contacts imported by mistake. For a customer's data deletion request, Klaviyo has a separate individual deletion process for privacy requests.

Unsubscribe

Unsubscribing is done by the recipient. Klaviyo records it as an Unsubscribed consent status; it is not shown as a suppression reason. With Klaviyo's global unsubscribe setting, which is on by default, someone who unsubscribes from one email is unsubscribed from all your email marketing.

What you can do about it: nothing, and that is the point. Removing a suppression does not resubscribe a profile that had unsubscribed.

Which one for list hygiene

For profiles that have never engaged, Klaviyo's guidance is to suppress. You keep the history, the profile no longer receives marketing email, and a mistake can be corrected. The steps are in Klaviyo list hygiene by hand.

TierSaver only ever suppresses. It does not delete profiles and does not change consent.
