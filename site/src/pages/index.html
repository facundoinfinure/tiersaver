How it works

Five steps. You approve the one that matters.

Klaviyo recommends suppressing profiles that have never engaged. TierSaver does it every month, with your Shopify orders as the safety check.

  1. 01Audit

    See where your list stands

    Connect Klaviyo and run a free, read-only audit. It shows how many active profiles you have and how many of them meet the cleanup criteria.

  2. 02Simulate

    Preview every cleanup

    A simulation lists how many profiles would be suppressed and why, with a sample you can review. Nothing runs without a simulation you approved in the last {{sim.ttlHours}} hours.

  3. 03Protect buyers

    Keep everyone who ever showed interest

    A profile is only a candidate if every one of these is true. Purchases are checked against both your Shopify orders and Klaviyo.

    • Created {{criteria.ageDays}}+ days ago
    • Received {{criteria.emails}}+ emails
    • Never opened
    • Never clicked
    • Never visited
    • Never bought

    SMS subscribers, lists you protect and profiles with incomplete data are always kept.

  4. 04Suppress

    Run it every month

    You start the first cleanup yourself. After that TierSaver runs monthly and finishes before your plan renews, so Klaviyo's count is up to date. Scheduled runs wait for your approval unless you choose otherwise.

  5. 05Undo

    Correct any run

    If a run suppressed someone it should not have, undo restores exactly the profiles from that run. Restored profiles are left out of cleanups for the next {{criteria.undoLockDays}} days.

Questions

It touches your list, so ask the hard questions.

More detail in the help center.

Will I lose customers?

No one who buys is touched. By default a profile is only a candidate if it has never purchased, checked against both your Shopify orders and Klaviyo, and has never opened, clicked or visited. You approve a simulation before anything runs, and if something looks wrong afterwards you can undo the run.

See the cleanup criteria.

Is my data deleted?

No. TierSaver suppresses profiles; it does not delete them. Their history stays in Klaviyo.

What suppressing means in Klaviyo.

Doesn't Klaviyo already do this?

Klaviyo recommends suppressing profiles that never engaged and gives you the tools to do it by hand. TierSaver applies the same criteria every month, adds a check against your Shopify orders, shows you a simulation first and keeps a log so a run can be undone.

If you prefer to do it yourself, the manual steps are here.

What happens if I uninstall?

Profiles that were suppressed stay suppressed. You can remove the suppression from Klaviyo at any time.

TierSaver removes its access tokens when you uninstall and the rest of your store's data when Shopify sends its deletion request. What happens on uninstall.

Does a cleaner list change my Klaviyo plan?

Not by itself. Klaviyo's help center says that suppressed profiles do not count toward your email plan's active profile count. If your count drops, you can move to a smaller plan in Klaviyo's billing settings, or let Klaviyo's auto-downgrade option do it. TierSaver never changes your plan, and makes no promise about your Klaviyo invoice.

The monthly schedule and your Klaviyo plan.

Can I still email someone after they are suppressed?

No. A suppressed profile cannot receive marketing email from your Klaviyo account, even if that person later buys. That is why the default criteria only select profiles that have never engaged in any way.

What does TierSaver store about my customers?

Klaviyo profile IDs and one-way hashes of email addresses, so it can recognize buyers and undo a run. It does not store customer email addresses, names or order contents. Access tokens are encrypted.
