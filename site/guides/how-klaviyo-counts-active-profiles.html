How Klaviyo counts active profiles

Klaviyo's email plans are sized by active profiles. Here is what that term covers, when the count is taken and what changes it.

Last updatedNovember 26, 2026

What counts as an active profile

Klaviyo's definition: any profile that can be emailed through Klaviyo, regardless of consent status, is an active profile. In practice that is a profile with an email address that is not suppressed from email.

That includes two groups:

  • Subscribers, who explicitly opted in to email marketing.
  • Profiles added by general engagement, such as someone who entered their email at checkout without opting in to marketing.

What does and does not count

ProfileCounts toward the Profiles and Email plan?
Has an email address, not suppressedYes, whether or not they subscribed
Suppressed from emailNo, once the suppression is processed. The profile stays in your account.
Unsubscribed from emailNo
DeletedNo. The profile is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
Has no email address (for example, a phone number only)No
Unsubscribed from SMS only, still emailableYes

One exception Klaviyo points out: on its Advanced KDP plan, billing is based on total profiles, including suppressed profiles. Klaviyo also says that a lower active profile count does not necessarily lower your bill.

When Klaviyo takes the count

  • Paid plans renew every month on the date your account started paying.
  • Your plan has to cover the number of active profiles you have at the end of your billing cycle.
  • You can see your cycle dates under Billing, on the Overview tab, and your Active and Suppressed counts under Audience, Profiles.
  • Klaviyo updates its count on its own schedule; its help center gives no fixed time for the count to change after a bulk suppression. With auto-downgrade on, it checks the count shortly before your billing cycle renews.

How plan sizes follow the count

  • Growing. If you have more active profiles than your plan allows at the end of a cycle, Klaviyo moves you to a plan that covers them for the next cycle. This does not depend on your upgrade preference: that setting is about sending limits.
  • Shrinking. Klaviyo states that a plan does not automatically move down just because you have fewer profiles. You can choose a smaller plan yourself after your count updates, or enable auto-downgrade based on profiles. With auto-downgrade on, Klaviyo checks your count shortly before the cycle renews.
  • Timing. A plan change takes effect in the next billing cycle, and it does not change your renewal date.

Keeping the count accurate

An active profile count is only meaningful if it reflects people you can still reach. Klaviyo calls the work of keeping it that way active profile management: suppressing profiles that have shown a long, sustained period of inactivity. It also gives two cautions:

  • Suppressing profiles too early, when they might still come back, can cost you revenue, because you can no longer email them.
  • A suppressed profile stays suppressed even if that person later starts a checkout or buys.

The practical steps are in Klaviyo list hygiene by hand.
