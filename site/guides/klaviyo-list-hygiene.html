Klaviyo list hygiene: how to find and suppress profiles that never engaged

Klaviyo documents how to find and suppress profiles that have never engaged. These are the steps, by hand. No app required.

Last updatedNovember 26, 2026

What list hygiene means here

Klaviyo distinguishes two jobs that are often mixed up:

  • Everyday list cleaning, for deliverability. Exclude recently unengaged subscribers from your regular campaigns, for example people who received at least 5 emails and have not opened or clicked in 90 days. Klaviyo's advice is to exclude them with a segment, not to suppress them.
  • Active profile management. Suppress profiles that have shown a long, sustained period of inactivity: no site visits, no purchases, no engagement with your emails. This guide is about this second job.

Before you start

  • Suppression is a decision to stop emailing someone. Klaviyo warns that suppressing too early can cost revenue, and that a suppressed profile stays suppressed even if that person later starts a checkout or buys.
  • Try to win them back first. Klaviyo's advice is to run long-inactive profiles through a last sunset flow before suppressing them.
  • Know what stops and what does not. Suppressed profiles stop receiving campaigns and most flow emails. Flows that Klaviyo has approved as transactional still send, and SMS and push are not affected.
  • Suppress, do not delete. Deleting cannot be undone. See suppress, delete or unsubscribe.
  • Check your role. Owners, Admins and Managers can suppress profiles.

Step 1. Find the profiles that never engaged

Klaviyo offers three ways.

Build an inactivity segment

Klaviyo's article "Understanding active profile management in Klaviyo" describes a segment that combines all of these conditions:

  • Person can receive email marketing
  • Email is set
  • Created at least 180 days ago
  • Has received at least 5 emails in the last 72 weeks
  • Has opened email 0 times over all time
  • Has clicked email 0 times over all time
  • Has been active on site 0 times over all time
  • Has viewed product 0 times over all time
  • Has started checkout 0 times over all time
  • Has placed order 0 times over all time

Creating the segment does not suppress anyone.

Use the profiles Klaviyo suggests

On the Suppressed profiles page (Audience, Profiles, View suppressed profiles), Klaviyo may show a banner that suggests profiles to suppress. The criteria Klaviyo publishes for it, in "How to manage email suppressions and delete profiles in bulk": can receive email marketing with no suppression added in the last 90 days, created at least 365 days ago, received 5 or more emails over roughly the last 72 weeks, never opened or clicked, and no recorded site visits, product views, started checkouts or purchases.

The two articles use different ages: 180 days in the segment above, 365 days here. TierSaver's default criteria follow this second set, with 365 days.

Use "Suppress inactive profiles" on a list or segment

When you bulk suppress a list or segment, Klaviyo may offer to suppress only inactive profiles. That option keeps active anyone in the group who is subscribed, has not marked your emails as spam, and has clicked an email or placed an order within the last 182 days. Klaviyo says the option is unavailable when your deliverability score for the last 30 days is 60 or lower, or when more than 120,000 profiles qualify as inactive.

Step 2. Check the list against real buyers

This step is not in Klaviyo's instructions, and it is the one that protects revenue. The conditions above rely on the activity Klaviyo has recorded for each profile. Your store's order history is an independent check: export the segment, compare it with your orders, and keep anyone who has bought from you.

Step 3. Suppress

  1. Go to Audience, then Lists & segments.
  2. Open the action menu next to the list or segment and select "Suppress current members".
  3. Review the counts. If Klaviyo offers the choice, pick "Suppress inactive profiles" or "Suppress everyone".
  4. Confirm. Processing may take some time.

Two things to keep in mind. The action applies to the current members only; profiles that join the segment later are not suppressed. And suppressing affects email only: SMS and push eligibility do not change.

Step 4. Keep a record

Before you confirm, export the segment and keep the file with the date and the conditions you used. If you later find you suppressed someone you should not have, that file tells you exactly whom to restore. Klaviyo lets you remove manual suppressions one profile at a time or in bulk for a list or segment.

Remember the waiting period: once a profile is suppressed and then unsuppressed, Klaviyo does not let it be manually suppressed again until 90 days after the previous suppression.

Step 5. Repeat every month

Klaviyo's advice is to manage your active profiles on an ongoing basis. A bulk suppression does not repeat by itself, so put it in your calendar. Klaviyo gives no fixed time for its active profile count to update after a bulk suppression: it updates the count on its own schedule and checks it shortly before your billing cycle renews. On Klaviyo's Advanced KDP plan, billing is based on total profiles, including suppressed profiles. How Klaviyo counts active profiles explains the rest.
