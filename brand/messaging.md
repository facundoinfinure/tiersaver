# TierSaver messaging guide

> **Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-25** (the version revised after the policy review). Source: `docs/03-MARCA.md`. The wording rules in `docs/12-CUMPLIMIENTO-KLAVIYO.md`, section 3.2, are binding and win over anything here.

## Name

- Parent brand: **TierSaver**.
- Product: **TierSaver: List Hygiene for Klaviyo** (descriptor decided by the owner on 2026-11-25). "for Klaviyo" goes at the end, so a third party's brand is never used as our own name. The Shopify App Store name field is limited to 30 characters, so the listing name is **TierSaver for Klaviyo**, with "list hygiene" in the subtitle.
- "Klaviyo" appears only as descriptive text about compatibility. Never Klaviyo's logo, typeface or colors. Never "official", "certified", "recommended by" or "Klaviyo partner", and never "recommended" or "recommends" next to Klaviyo's name: the criteria are "the criteria Klaviyo publishes for suppressing unengaged profiles".
- In the Shopify App Store, "tier" is often read as a volume discount. The listing subtitle and the first headline on the site must say what the product does, with no ambiguity: list hygiene for Klaviyo.

## Positioning

Automatic list hygiene for Klaviyo. TierSaver is not a deliverability tool and not an email marketing tool.

- **For**: Shopify brands whose Klaviyo lists are full of profiles that never engaged.
- **Promise**: keep the list clean with no manual work, without touching anyone who buys.
- **How**: it audits, simulates, checks every profile against Shopify orders, and suppresses the profiles that meet the criteria Klaviyo publishes for suppressing unengaged profiles, once you approve. It runs monthly and finishes before your plan renews.
- **Why believe it**: it uses the criteria Klaviyo publishes by default; nothing is touched without an approved simulation; buyers are protected with Shopify order data; every run is logged and can be undone; nothing is deleted; the price is flat, and a month with no cleanup is not charged.

## What the default criteria are

Use this wording when a page explains who gets suppressed. By default, a profile is suppressed only if all of these are true:

- It was created at least 365 days ago.
- It received 5 or more emails over roughly the last 72 weeks.
- It never opened or clicked an email.
- It has no recorded site visit, product view or started checkout.
- It never purchased, in your Shopify orders or in Klaviyo.

Profiles with SMS consent, profiles in the lists and segments you mark as protected, and profiles with incomplete data always stay active. A wider mode is opt-in. It is TierSaver's own optional setting: it goes beyond the criteria Klaviyo publishes and suppresses more profiles. Never call it "stricter", which can be read backwards. Its limits cannot be lowered: purchases 365 days, clicks, opens and site activity 182 days, profile age 180 days.

## Pricing

Prices belong in the pricing section of the listing and on the pricing part of the site, nowhere else in the listing.

- A flat monthly price by list size: $15 up to 10,000 active profiles, $29 up to 25,000, $79 up to 75,000, $149 up to 150,000. Larger lists: by agreement. Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-25.
- A billing cycle with no cleanup is not charged. The audit is free. Billing goes through the merchant's Shopify invoice.
- The price never depends on the merchant's Klaviyo invoice.

## Voice

- Plain English, short sentences, numbers with context.
- Honest: no promise about what the merchant pays Klaviyo. If a page shows the plan a list would land on, it is secondary, it comes from the merchant's own data, and it says "estimated".
- Calm about what is delicate. The app touches the merchant's list, so the tone is control, not urgency.
- No exclamation marks. No growth jargon.
- Talk about Klaviyo's plan the way Klaviyo does: suppressed profiles do not count toward the plan, according to Klaviyo's own help documentation. No comparison that makes Klaviyo look bad.
- Never invent a number. Any figure a merchant sees comes from their own data or is labelled as an estimate.

## Words and framings never to use

- **avoid**, **dodge**, **beat** (or **circumvent**, **get around**, **game**, **outsmart**) when talking about Klaviyo's pricing, bills or fees.
- Paying Klaviyo less as a headline, subtitle or main keyword, in any form: "stop paying Klaviyo for…", or "lower", "reduce" or "cut" next to "your Klaviyo bill".
- The timing of a run against billing as a promise: "right before your billing date", "before Klaviyo counts them", "against your billing date".
- "pays for itself"; "you only pay when Klaviyo confirms a lower tier"; "we take 20% of what you save"; any wording that ties our price to what the merchant pays Klaviyo.
- Suppressing and reactivating in the same promise. TierSaver does not reactivate profiles automatically.
- Figures, percentages or guarantees about money in the Shopify listing: none, real or estimated. On the site, only as an estimate from the merchant's own data.
- "official", "certified", "recommended by", "Klaviyo partner", and Klaviyo's logo.
- "Klaviyo's own recommended criteria", "Klaviyo recommends" or any other pairing of Klaviyo's name with "recommended" (Klaviyo's listing rules). Say "the criteria Klaviyo publishes for suppressing unengaged profiles".
- "read-only" for the audit. It changes no profile, but it creates one segment in the merchant's Klaviyo account.
- Comparisons that make Klaviyo look bad, such as "Klaviyo charges you for dead profiles".
- **delete** for what the app does. The app suppresses; it never deletes profiles.
- Exclamation marks, urgency ("act now", "last chance"), and growth jargon ("hack", "10x", "skyrocket", "supercharge").
- "deliverability tool" or "email marketing tool" as a description of TierSaver.

## Objections and answers

Draft answers for the site and the help center.

**Will I lose customers?**
By default, TierSaver only suppresses profiles that never opened, clicked, visited or purchased. Every profile is checked against your Shopify orders, and you approve a simulation before anything runs. If something looks wrong afterwards, you can restore the cleanup from the app, within the limits the help center describes.

**Is my data deleted?**
No. TierSaver suppresses profiles; it does not delete them. Their history stays in Klaviyo.

**Doesn't Klaviyo already do this?**
Klaviyo publishes criteria for suppressing unengaged profiles and gives you the tools to do it by hand. TierSaver applies those criteria for you every month, checks each profile against your Shopify orders, shows you a simulation first, and keeps a log.

**What if a suppressed profile buys later?**
The profile stays suppressed in Klaviyo until the person resubscribes or you remove the suppression by hand. TierSaver does not change that on its own.

**What happens if I uninstall?**
Profiles that were suppressed stay suppressed. You can remove a suppression from Klaviyo at any time.

**How does pricing work?**
A flat monthly price based on the size of your list, billed through Shopify. A month with no cleanup is not charged, and the audit is free.
