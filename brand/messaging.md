# TierSaver messaging guide

> **Status: draft, pending the owner's approval.** Nothing here is final until it is approved in Phase 2. Source: `docs/03-MARCA.md`. Do not publish copy from this file before approval.

## Name

- Parent brand: **TierSaver**.
- Product: **TierSaver for Klaviyo**. "for Klaviyo" goes at the end, so a third party's brand is never used as our own name.
- Naming rules still have to be confirmed against the current Shopify App Store requirements and Klaviyo's brand guidelines.
- In the Shopify App Store, "tier" is often read as a volume discount. The listing subtitle and the first headline on the site must say "Klaviyo bill" with no ambiguity.

## Positioning

Automatic list hygiene for Klaviyo, with savings as a measurable result. TierSaver is not a deliverability tool and not an email marketing tool.

- **For**: Shopify brands whose Klaviyo lists are full of profiles that stopped engaging.
- **Promise**: keep the list healthy with no manual work, without touching anyone who buys.
- **How**: it audits, simulates, checks every profile against Shopify orders, suppresses before the billing date, and reactivates anyone who buys again.
- **Why believe it**: nothing is touched without a simulation; buyers are protected with Shopify data; everything can be undone; you are only charged when Klaviyo confirms a lower tier.

## Voice

- Plain English, short sentences, numbers with context.
- Honest: every savings figure says "estimated" until the merchant sees it on their own invoice.
- Calm about what is delicate. The app touches the merchant's list, so the tone is control, not urgency.
- No exclamation marks. No growth jargon.
- Savings are a consequence of a healthy list: suppressed profiles do not count toward the plan, according to Klaviyo's own help documentation. Say it that way.
- Never invent a number. Any figure a merchant sees comes from their own data or is labelled as an estimate.

## Words and framings never to use

- **avoid**, **dodge**, **beat** (or **circumvent**, **get around**, **game**, **outsmart**) when talking about Klaviyo's pricing, bills or fees.
- "Pay Klaviyo less" as the whole pitch. Lead with list hygiene; the lower bill is the result.
- **delete** for what the app does. The app suppresses; it never deletes profiles.
- Savings stated as a fact before the invoice shows them. Use "estimated".
- "guaranteed savings". The bill *can* drop a tier; if it does not, there is no charge.
- Exclamation marks, urgency ("act now", "last chance"), and growth jargon ("hack", "10x", "skyrocket", "supercharge").
- "deliverability tool" or "email marketing tool" as a description of TierSaver.

## Objections and answers

Draft answers for the site and the help center.

**Will I lose customers?**
No one who buys is touched. Protection rules keep buyers active, every profile is checked against your Shopify orders, and you approve a simulation before anything runs. If something looks wrong afterwards, you can undo the run.

**Is my data deleted?**
No. TierSaver suppresses profiles; it does not delete them. Their history stays in Klaviyo.

**Doesn't Klaviyo already do this?**
Klaviyo recommends suppressing unengaged profiles and lets you do it by hand. It does not schedule it against your billing date. TierSaver does.

**What happens if I uninstall?**
Profiles that were suppressed stay suppressed. You can reactivate them from Klaviyo at any time.

## Checklist before publishing any copy

- Does the first line a merchant reads say "Klaviyo" and what the product does?
- Is every savings figure real merchant data or labelled "estimated"?
- Is the lower bill presented as the result of a healthy list?
- Does it say "suppress", not "delete"?
- No exclamation marks, no urgency, none of the words above?
